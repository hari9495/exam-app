01

Foundations

Workfox is an assessment and hiring platform by Yukthix Consulting. The visual language is "Workfox Azure": professional polish with examination-grade discipline — detail over decoration, ornament only when it encodes data, color only when it means something.

Personality

  • Precise: every number is exact, tabular, and sourced.
  • Calm: the interface never shouts; urgency is reserved for genuine integrity events.
  • Watchful: live states are visibly live — the platform sees what happens in a session.
  • Professional: a tool teams live in all day, not a landing page.

Naming

The product is Workfox (status: working name) by Yukthix Consulting. The product name renders only from the BRAND constant in code.

  • Product surfaces where the platform speaks (staff login, footers, transactional email) say Workfox.
  • Legal and billing contexts say Yukthix Consulting.
  • On candidate-facing org-branded surfaces the org speaks: its name and color lead; Workfox appears only as "powered by Workfox" attribution where contractually shown.

02

Voice and tone

An invigilator, not a cheerleader. State the fact, then the next step. Short sentences. No exclamation marks in system copy.

Register

  • Staff-facing: institutional and exact.
  • Candidate-facing: warm, plain, and reassuring — an exam is stressful enough.
  • Buttons: verb first, 1–3 words ("Release results", "Invite candidates").
  • Errors: what happened, then what to do. Never blame, never apologize twice.

Examples

Do — That email or password isn't right.

Don't — Oops! Something went wrong!

Do — Results released to 41 candidates.

Don't — Success!! Your results have been released successfully!

Do — Camera lost. Check your connection — the timer is paused.

Don't — ERROR: MediaStreamTrack ended unexpectedly.

Do — Invite candidates

Don't — Click here to get started

03

Logo

The working mark is a stroke-drawn W monogram plus the Workfox wordmark set in Bricolage Grotesque 600. Both are placeholders with full usage rules, so the final mark can drop in without relayout.

Workfox working mark · replace in WorkfoxMark.tsx

Usage

  • Monogram minimum size 14px; wordmark minimum 12px cap height.
  • Clear space: half the mark height on all sides.
  • The mark inherits currentColor: ink on light, foreground on dark, org-on-primary inside org-colored tiles.
  • Never stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, or gradient the mark.
  • On org-branded candidate surfaces the org logo leads; the mark appears only in attribution.

Product tokens

  • Accent = the slot: primary actions, focus, active nav. An org's color replaces it wholesale on branded surfaces.
  • A red 3px attention rail marks flagged items; nothing else may use it.
  • Status colors (clear / review / flagged) never re-tint under white-labeling.
  • Dark mode is flat navy — depth comes from borders, not elevation.

05

Typography

Bricolage Grotesque carries identity moments; Hanken Grotesk carries the interface; the mono stack carries evidence — serials, scores, clocks, counts.

How Workfox reads

Eyebrow · uppercase · accent

Body copy is Hanken Grotesk — clean, quiet, and legible at 14–15px with a generous line height for long working sessions.

WF-00412-003 · 94/100 · 00:41:22

Scale

  • Display (Bricolage 600, −0.025em): page and job titles 26–34px.
  • Eyebrow: 10.5–11px, 600, +0.14em, uppercase, accent color.
  • UI labels: 12px/600 muted. Body: 13–14px. Helper: 11–12.5px.
  • Mono: serials, scores, timers, counts — always tabular figures.

Rules

  • Never letter-space body text; only eyebrows and mono micro-labels.
  • One display moment per screen. Everything else is Hanken.

Components

Standards

  • Fields: 38px, 1px hair border, 6px radius; focus = accent border + 18% ring.
  • Primary button: accent fill (the slot), 40px, 6px radius, weight 600.
  • Cards: paper on surface grounds, 1px hair border, 8px radius, shadow subtle and at most two layers.
  • Flagged rows and cards carry the red attention rail.
  • Every decorative metric must be data-true (funnel widths from real counts, rings from real scores).

21st.dev intake

  • Strip hardcoded colors to tokens. Strip gradients unless data-true. Match radii to 6/8px.
  • If a component fights the accent-slot rule, adapt it or reject it.

07

Motion and interaction

One considered entrance per surface; micro-feedback on press; reduced motion always honored.

  • Page and card entrance: a single fade plus 8px rise, about 0.4s, ease-out. No stagger parades.
  • Press: scale 0.98 spring tap on primary actions.
  • Live states may pulse subtly (watch dots); nothing else animates idly.
  • Reduced motion is respected on every surface — no exceptions.
  • Focus visibility is non-negotiable: every interactive element shows the slot-colored ring.

08

Email and marketing basics

Transactional email is part of the product: same voice, same restraint. Marketing pages may breathe more but draw from the same tokens.

  • Email chrome: white ground, ink text, one accent-colored button at most.
  • Sender identity: the org for candidate mail ("sent via Workfox" in the footer); Workfox for staff and system mail.
  • Legal footer: Yukthix Consulting, plus unsubscribe or preferences where applicable.
  • Subject lines: fact first, no urgency theatre ("Your results for Drive 07", not "Don't miss your results!").
