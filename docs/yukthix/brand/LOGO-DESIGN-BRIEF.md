# YukthiX logo design brief (for the commissioned designer)

> Issued 27 Sep 2026 by the brand owner. Read with [BRAND-GUIDELINES.md](BRAND-GUIDELINES.md) Parts 1 and 3. Budget band ₹50,000–3,00,000 depending on studio; two rounds of revision; all rights assigned to the company on payment.

## 1. Who we are
YukthiX is an all-in-one HR, hiring and assessment platform from India, priced at $1 per person a month with everything included. Products: **YukthiX HR**, **YukthiX Hire**, **YukthiX Assess**, **YukthiX Analytics**. Promise: **"Everything. Included."** Personality: straightforward, confident, warm, precise. Audience: founders and HR teams of small and mid-size Indian companies, staffing agencies, colleges, then global SMBs. Buyers must feel a low price does **not** mean a weak product.

## 2. The name
**YukthiX**, pronounced YOOK-thee-eks. From *yukti* (Sanskrit: reason, strategy, skill) + X (the unknown, the multiplier, every product to come). Capital Y and X, no space, no hyphen. The X is the natural hero of the mark.

## 3. What to deliver
1. **Primary wordmark** "YukthiX", custom-lettered or heavily customised from a geometric grotesque; must sit comfortably beside IBM Plex Sans, the product and brand typeface (design system §2, changed 28 Sep 2026).
2. **X monogram**, derived from the wordmark's X, that works alone at 16 px (favicon), 48 px (app icon), and large (signage). Android adaptive icon and iOS icon versions with safe zones.
3. **Lockups:** horizontal and stacked; product lockups "YukthiX HR / Hire / Assess / Analytics" (descriptor in a consistent secondary weight); "Powered by YukthiX" small lockup for footers; "YukthiX Verified Partner" badge.
4. **Colour versions:** Azure `#3B5FE3` on white; Ink `#0B1220` mono; white reversed on Azure and on Ink; single-colour for print and embroidery.
5. **Rules sheet:** clear space (defined by the x-height), minimum sizes, placement on photography, misuse examples (stretch, recolour, effects, outline, rotation, busy backgrounds).
6. **Print values:** CMYK and Pantone for Azure and Ink.
7. **Files:** SVG masters; PNG at 1×, 2×, 3×; PDF; ICO / ICNS for icons; named `yukthix-logo-{variant}-{colour}.{ext}`; a one-page "how to use" PDF.
8. **Optional (quote separately):** a short logo animation (≤ 1.5 s, reduced-motion variant) for app launch and video intros.

## 4. Direction
- **Confident, geometric, calm.** Comparable in spirit to how Stripe, Atlassian, Razorpay and Notion feel: simple forms, no gimmicks, ages well.
- **Distinctive X.** Explore the X as intersection, multiplier or checkmark-like affirmation ("included"). Avoid an X that reads as "close" or "wrong".
- **Works tiny.** Most impressions will be a 16–48 px icon on phones and a footer "Powered by".
- **Neutral culturally.** Indian origin is in the name; do not add mandalas, lotuses, maps, flags or Devanagari styling to the mark.
- **One colour.** The mark must work in a single colour; gradients are not part of the logo.

## 5. Do not
- Reuse or adapt anything from Workfox (the previous brand), Frappe, or any competitor (Keka, greytHR, Darwinbox, Zoho, HackerRank, Greenhouse).
- Use stock or AI-generated marks, clip-art, or a font's letters unmodified (licence and distinctiveness).
- Use people, clocks, calendars, briefcases, gears, handshakes, or "HR" clichés.
- Use red, orange or yellow as the primary (reserved for status meanings in the product).

## 6. Process
1. Kick-off call, brand guidelines walkthrough (1 hour).
2. Round 1: three directions as wordmark + monogram, shown at 16 px, 48 px, 200 px and on a phone footer.
3. Brand owner picks one; round 2 refines; trademark counsel checks distinctiveness before final.
4. Final delivery with the rules sheet and files; hand-over to the design system owner who encodes the mark and colours in `packages/ui`.

## 7. Acceptance checklist
- Legible at 16 px and in single colour.
- Clear space and minimum sizes documented.
- Distinct from competitor marks (counsel confirms).
- All files and formats delivered and named per §3.
- Rights assignment signed.

## 8. Add-on: custom icon set (added 28 Sep 2026, design system §8)
Quote separately: a custom YukthiX icon set in the same visual grammar as the monogram — 1.5 px outline on a 24 px grid, optical sizes 16 / 20 / 24. First batch (~40): payslip, attendance punch, leave, statutory filing, proctoring, offer letter, org unit, rule builder, workflow, approvals, candidate, test, invigilator, contract worker, and other HR concepts. Later batches replace the general Lucide set. Delivered as SVG with a naming convention and a usage sheet.

## 9. Add-on: 4 colour composites (added 7 Oct 2026, design system §8)
Separate from the outline set above. Four colour icons in the **Fluent UI System Icons Color** style, built from Fluent's own MIT Color parts (github.com/microsoft/fluentui-system-icons): same grid, soft gradients and sizes 20 / 24 / 32 / 48, with a **16 px badge bottom-right** like Fluent's send-clock, star-settings and people-sync (base art cut back around the badge).
- **Payslip** = document-text + coin-multiple badge.
- **Attendance punch** = person + clock badge.
- **Offer letter** = mail + ribbon badge (or briefcase).
- **Workflow** = clipboard-task + people-sync badge.

Deliver SVG per size named `ic_yx_<name>_<size>_color.svg`, checked on white and on the dark surface `#111A2B`. They replace today's stand-ins (Receipt, Shifts, Mail, ApprovalsApp).
