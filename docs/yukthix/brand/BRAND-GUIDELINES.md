# YukthiX Brand Guidelines — master document (v1.0)

> **Status:** v1.0, 27 Sep 2026. All ten founder decisions (Q1–Q10) are taken and recorded in Part 9. Every section below exists in the brand books of large companies (Apple, Google, IBM, Atlassian, Salesforce, Zoho, Razorpay). Items marked **team action** need a designer, lawyer or engineer to produce the asset; the rule for it is already written here. Companion: [LOGO-DESIGN-BRIEF.md](LOGO-DESIGN-BRIEF.md).
>
> **Why a small company needs this:** the market analysis shows buyers distrust products that look inconsistent or cheap, and the $1 price already invites that doubt. Consistent brand is the cheapest proof of quality we have.
>
> **How this connects to existing work:** the visual system is the shared `packages/ui` (spec D16, UI brief §2); the product name and rename are D12; tenant branding rules are P02 / UI brief principle 10; legal artefacts are APX-G.

## Part 1 · Brand foundation

### 1.1 Purpose (why we exist)
**Decided (Q1):** *Every company, however small, deserves the HR, hiring and assessment tools that only large companies could afford.*

### 1.2 Mission (what we do)
**Decided (Q1):** *We build one platform for the whole employee journey, from the first test to the last payslip, priced so any company can use all of it.*

### 1.3 Vision (where we are going)
**Decided (Q1):** *The default HR platform for India's companies, then for the world's.*

### 1.4 Values (how we behave) — **decided (Q2), all six**
| Value | What it means in practice | Where it already shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Everything included** | No tiers, no hidden fees, no "contact sales" | D18, YX-BILL-13/14 |
| **Your rules, not ours** | Every policy configurable; the law is a floor | D17, D19, P19 |
| **Fair by design** | No auto-fail, evidence-based appeals, fairness reports | T04, T05, A13 |
| **Correct on payroll day** | Pre-run checks, variance review, a human on payroll day | M03, P14 |
| **Leave when you like** | Full export any time, deletion certificate | YX-TEN-03/05 |
| **Plain words** | We explain every result ("why did I get this?") | YX-RULE-10 |

### 1.5 Positioning statement (one sentence for ourselves, not customers)
**Decided (Q3).** Formula: *For [audience] who [need], YukthiX is the [category] that [key benefit], unlike [alternatives] which [weakness].*
*For Indian SMBs and staffing firms that pay for seats they don't have and features they can't switch on, YukthiX is the all-in-one HR, hiring and assessment platform where everything is included at $1 per person, unlike tiered HR suites that charge for every module.*

### 1.6 Brand promise (one line for customers)
**Decided (Q3): "Everything. Included."**
- Always written with both full stops and capital E and I. In body copy the phrase "everything included" may be used normally.
- Supporting lines (body copy and decks, not taglines): *HR, hiring and assessment. One price, no surprises.* · *Built for the people you actually have.*
- In Hindi, Tamil and Telugu the promise is transcreated by a native copywriter and approved by the brand owner; the English line stays beside the logo.

### 1.7 Brand personality (how we sound and look) — **decided (Q4)**
| We are | But never |
|---|---|
| **Straightforward** | blunt or cold |
| **Confident** | arrogant or dismissive of competitors |
| **Warm** | cute or jokey about pay, jobs or exams |
| **Precise** | bureaucratic or jargon-filled |

### 1.8 Audiences
| Audience | What they care about | Proof they want |
|---|---|---|
| Founders / CFOs of 5–200-person companies | Price, no surprises, payroll correctness | Public price list, cost calculator, pilot customers |
| HR and payroll admins | Payroll day support, configurability, less manual work | Setup hub, rule builder, support hours |
| Staffing agency owners | One system from requirement to GST invoice | Agency stack, vendor portal |
| Recruiters and hiring managers | Speed, scorecards, candidate experience | Scheduling, mobile |
| Candidates and test-takers | Fairness, privacy, clear status | Consent, appeals, "why" panel |
| Employees | Payslips, leave, check-in that works | Mobile app quality |
| CA firms and partners | Cross-client console, commission | P16 |
| Investors | Traction, proof, multi-product vision | Metrics, certifications |

### 1.9 Brand architecture — **decided (Q5): master brand + descriptor**
| Product | Name | One-line descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| HRMS + payroll | **YukthiX HR** | People, leave, attendance, payroll and statutory, all included |
| ATS + staffing | **YukthiX Hire** | Recruiting, staffing desk, client and vendor portals |
| Proctoring + assessment | **YukthiX Assess** | Tests, proctoring, interviews, evaluation |
| Analytics | **YukthiX Analytics** | Included with every product; named only when sold as a capability |
| Whole platform | **YukthiX** (or "the YukthiX platform") | Never "YukthiX HR Suite" in public copy; that is the internal project name |

Rules: the product name is always the two words together ("YukthiX Hire", never "Hire" alone in public copy). Descriptors stay in English in every language. Other named things:
- Partner programme: **YukthiX Partners**; badge: **YukthiX Verified Partner**.
- Developer platform: **YukthiX Developers** (docs, API, sandbox).
- Secure exam client (T08): **YukthiX Secure Client** (descriptive, no separate brand).
- Help centre: **YukthiX Help**; status page: **status.yukthix.com**.
- Internal codes (YX-…, P01, M03, T04) never appear in customer-facing text.

### 1.10 Name — **decided (Q6)**
- **Spelling:** YukthiX (capital Y, capital X, no space, no hyphen). Never "Yukthix", "YukthiX HR Suite" in running text after first mention, never "YX" in customer-facing text (YX is the internal rule prefix).
- **Pronunciation:** **YOOK-thee-eks**. Record an audio clip for the press kit and the about page. The brand is always written in Latin script; Indic transliterations are for spoken reference only.
- **Meaning / story (approved wording):** *YukthiX comes from* yukti, *the Sanskrit word for reason, strategy and skill, and X, the unknown that stands for every product still to come and for the multiplier we want to be for the companies that use us. Smart strategy, for every company.*
- **Never** explain the name as a pun and never split it into "Yukthi X".
- **Rename from Workfox:** at public launch (D16); pilot shows Workfox. A rename checklist is in Part 4.

## Part 2 · Verbal identity

### 2.1 Voice (constant) and tone (varies by situation)
Voice follows the personality in 1.7. Tone matrix:
| Situation | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing site | Confident, plain, specific numbers | "₹96 a person. Everything included." |
| Product UI | Calm, short, action-first | "Payroll approved. Bank file ready." |
| Errors | Own the problem, say what to do | "We couldn't reach your bank. Nothing was sent. Try again or use NEFT." |
| Payroll and money | Precise, never playful | Show the number, the rule and the date |
| Proctoring and candidates | Respectful, neutral, never accusatory | "This session has a review flag" not "cheating detected" |
| Legal and privacy | Plain, complete, no marketing | See APX-G |
| Support | Human, fast, first-person plural | "We've fixed it. Here's what changed." |

### 2.2 Writing rules
- Short sentences, active voice, one idea per sentence.
- Numbers as numerals; Indian formatting (₹1,00,000) in India, local formatting elsewhere.
- Never "users": say employees, candidates, recruiters, admins.
- Never "cheating" in product text: say "flag", "review", "signal".
- Never "tiers", "plans", "premium", "upgrade" (there is one plan, D18).
- Say "included" not "free"; say "add-on" only for the D18 list.
- Dates: 27 Sep 2026 style in product; ISO in exports.
- Capitalisation: sentence case everywhere, including buttons and headings.

### 2.3 Terminology (single source: the product glossary)
One term per concept, used in UI, docs, sales and support. Seed list: employee, employment, legal entity, location, pay period, payslip, leave type, check-in, regularisation, request, approval, policy, rule, starter template, test, attempt, flag, review, appeal, invigilator, proctor, recruiter, hiring manager, candidate, application, requirement (staffing), client, vendor, partner, workspace (company account). Banned synonyms listed beside each.

### 2.4 Product naming and UI labels
Follow 1.9. Feature names are descriptive and lower case ("rule builder", "setup hub"), not trademarked sub-brands, unless Q5 says otherwise.

### 2.5 Tagline usage
Where the promise (1.6) appears: site header, deck cover, email footer, app store. Never inside the product UI.

### 2.6 Languages and localisation
- English (India) is the source language; Hindi, Tamil, Telugu at launch (P04); more per market.
- Translations are reviewed by a native speaker; brand names stay in Latin script; the promise is transcreated, not translated literally.
- Indic-script fallback fonts are specified in 3.3.

### 2.7 Inclusive and fair language
- People-first, gender-neutral defaults ("they"), no assumptions about family, religion or region.
- Disability, caste, religion and health are referred to only where a feature legally requires it, in the law's terms.
- Candidate-facing text never implies guilt; see 2.1.

### 2.8 Legal and compliance wording
Standard phrases for: GST invoices, consent screens, notices, "advisory score", "not legal advice", partner disclosures, data residency. Source: APX-G artefacts.

### 2.9 Boilerplate (about us)
**25 words:** YukthiX is the all-in-one HR, hiring and assessment platform where everything is included at $1 per person a month. Built in India for companies everywhere.

**50 words:** YukthiX gives companies of any size the HR, hiring and assessment tools that only large companies could afford. YukthiX HR, YukthiX Hire and YukthiX Assess run on one platform, with every feature included at $1 per person a month, no tiers and no hidden fees. Everything. Included.

**100 words:** YukthiX is an all-in-one people platform built in India for companies everywhere. YukthiX HR covers people, leave, attendance, payroll and statutory compliance. YukthiX Hire covers recruiting, staffing and client and vendor portals. YukthiX Assess covers tests, proctoring, interviews and evaluation. Every feature of every product is included at $1 per person a month, with a published price list, no tiers, no hidden fees and no seat minimums beyond a few dollars. Companies set their own rules, keep their data, and can leave with everything at any time. The name comes from *yukti*, Sanskrit for reason and strategy. Everything. Included.

## Part 3 · Visual identity

### 3.1 Logo — **decided (Q7): custom wordmark + X monogram, commissioned from a professional designer** (brief: [LOGO-DESIGN-BRIEF.md](LOGO-DESIGN-BRIEF.md); team action: appoint the designer)
System the designer must deliver: primary logo (wordmark), monogram / app icon, horizontal and stacked lockups, product lockups (per 1.9), partner "Powered by YukthiX" lockup, mono and reversed versions.
Rules to write once the mark exists: clear space (x-height based), minimum sizes (print 20 mm, screen 96 px wordmark; icon 16 px), backgrounds allowed, misuse list (no stretching, recolouring, effects, rotation, outlines, placing on busy photos), file formats (SVG master, PNG at 1× 2× 3×, PDF for print), naming convention `yukthix-logo-{variant}-{colour}.{ext}`.
**Clean-room rule:** the logo is an original commission. Nothing derived from Workfox, Frappe or any competitor.

### 3.2 Colour — **decided (Q8): Azure is the brand colour**
**DECIDED base (UI brief):** Azure accent `#3B5FE3`; ink `#0B1220`; muted `#64748B`; hairline `#E2E8F0`; surface `#F3F5F7`; paper `#FFFFFF`; status palette success / warning / danger / info / neutral / purple with background pairs, one meaning per colour. Tenant brand colour `--org-primary` overrides accent on tenant-facing surfaces.
**Brand palette v1.0**
| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | **Azure** | `#3B5FE3` | Logo, links, primary buttons, brand accents |
| Primary dark | Azure 700 | `#2A46B8` | Hover, pressed |
| Primary light | Azure 100 | `#DDE5FB` | Selected states, tints, illustration fills |
| Ink | Ink | `#0B1220` | Headings, body text on light |
| Muted | Slate | `#64748B` | Secondary text, captions |
| Hairline | Mist | `#E2E8F0` | Borders, dividers |
| Surface | Slate 25 | `#F7F8FA` | Page background |
| Paper | White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, sheets |
| Success / Warning / Danger / Info | from the UI status palette | tokens in `packages/ui` | Approved, pending, rejected, notices |

Rules: Azure is never used for danger or warning, and status colours are never decorative. The neutral scale has 10 steps (Ink to White) as tokens. The designer supplies CMYK and Pantone matches for Azure and Ink. A dark-mode set (Azure 300 `#8FA4F0` on Ink) is part of the design system. All text meets WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text and UI). The full ramps (Azure 50–800, Slate 0–900), status pairs, AI purple, the eight-colour chart palette, the sequential and diverging scales and the dark-mode tokens are defined in [DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](DESIGN-SYSTEM.md) §3 and §48, which is the single source for hex values. Gradients are allowed only as Azure to Azure 700 on marketing surfaces, never in the product UI.

### 3.3 Typography
**DECIDED (design system §2, 28 Sep 2026; replaces Bricolage Grotesque + Hanken Grotesk):** **IBM Plex Sans** for all text, IBM Plex Mono for IDs and codes, IBM Plex Sans Devanagari, Noto Sans Tamil and Telugu, IBM Plex Sans Arabic later. Self-hosted, OFL. Weights 400 / 500 / 600. Product scale 12 / 13 / 14 (body) / 16 / 18 / 22 / 28 / 36 px; tabular figures for money and tables. Full scale and density modes in [DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](DESIGN-SYSTEM.md) §2.
Still to specify for marketing and print: large display sizes, weights, line heights, letter spacing for the wordmark, numerals (tabular for money and tables), Indic fallbacks (Noto Sans Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu) and their vertical metrics, system fallbacks, minimum sizes (body 14 px web, 16 px mobile), print equivalents, email-safe stack (Arial / Helvetica), document fonts for letters and payslips (must render in LibreOffice PDF, P05).

### 3.4 Iconography
One icon set (stroke weight, corner radius, grid 24 px), outline for UI, filled for status. Source: the shared UI package. Product and module icons for navigation. Never mix sets. Clean-room: no Frappe / Workfox icons.

### 3.5 Illustration — **decided (Q9): flat geometric**
Rules: purpose (login, careers page and marketing; not on in-product empty states, per design system §1 / §9), people shown diverse and Indian-first, no stock clip-art, colour from the palette, consistent line weight.

### 3.6 Photography — **decided (Q9): real Indian workplaces**
Real workplaces in India (factory, office, field, campus), natural light, no staged handshakes, model releases on file, no competitor logos in frame. Screenshots use the demo tenant only, never real customer data (P02).

### 3.7 Data visualisation
Follows P09 and the UI brief: categorical palette (3.2), one meaning per colour, no 3D, no pie for more than 4 slices, tabular numerals, suppression notice when groups are small (P09).

### 3.8 Motion
Purposeful, 120–200 ms in the product (full rules and the five milestone delights in [DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](DESIGN-SYSTEM.md) §7), ease-out, reduced-motion respected; no motion on payroll or legal confirmations except state change; loading states over spinners where possible.

### 3.9 Layout and grid
8-px spacing scale, 12-column responsive grid, mobile-first (M04 tab map), print A4 grid for letters and payslips (P05 templates), slide grid 16:9.

### 3.10 Sound (optional, later)
Notification sound for the mobile app; none in payroll flows. Decide when M04 ships.

## Part 5 · Brand inside the product

### 5.1 Where the YukthiX brand appears — **decided (Q10): tenant brand on employee and candidate surfaces, "Powered by YukthiX" on by default, and the included white-label option removes it**
| Surface | Default | Tenant can change? |
|---|---|---|

| Candidate portal, careers page, test screen | Tenant brand; "Powered by YukthiX" small; the Secure Client installer, its integrity screen and the consent / privacy screens always carry the YukthiX brand so candidates know who processes their data | "Powered by" removable; consent and client screens are not |
| Letters, payslips, certificates | Tenant brand only | — |
| Emails to employees / candidates | Tenant sender name and logo | — |
| Emails to admins about billing, security, maintenance | YukthiX | No |
| Partner portal | Partner brand allowed (P16 Q5) | Yes |
| API docs, developer portal, status page | YukthiX | No |

### 5.2 Tenant brand controls
Logo (light / dark), primary colour with automatic contrast check, favicon, sender name, letterhead, careers page theme. Tokens: `--org-primary` and derived. Limits: tenant colour never replaces semantic status colours.

### 5.3 Design system as the single source
Tokens, components and patterns live in `packages/ui` with Storybook (D16). This document sets the brand rules; the design system implements them. When they conflict, this document wins and the design system is fixed.

### 5.4 Accessibility
WCAG 2.2 AA everywhere (T03 decision extends to the whole product; P21): contrast, focus states, keyboard, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, text scaling to 200 %, no colour-only meaning. External audit before launch (team action).

## Part 7 · Governance

### 7.1 Roles
- **Brand owner:** a founder until a marketing lead exists. Approves logos, colours, taglines, major campaigns, partner co-branding.
- **Design system owner:** the front-end lead. Owns tokens and components.
- **Writing owner:** owns the glossary and UX copy review.

### 7.2 Approval
What needs approval: anything public with the logo, any new colour or typeface, any tagline, any competitor comparison, any co-branding. Turnaround 2 working days.

### 7.3 Asset library
One folder (or DAM) with versioned masters: logos, fonts, templates (deck, doc, email, social), photos with licences, icon set, colour files (ASE, CSS tokens), the glossary. Read-only for everyone except owners.

### 7.4 Change process
Propose → brand owner review → update this document with a dated decision row → update the design system → announce. Version number bumps on every change; a changelog sits at the end of this document.

### 7.5 Review cadence
Quarterly brand review; a full audit of every application before public launch and once a year.

### 7.6 Measurement
Brand health signals: unprompted recall in the target segments, website conversion, support tickets about confusing wording, accessibility audit results, consistency audit score.

## Part 8 · Checklists

**Before anything goes public:** correct logo file from the library · clear space and minimum size · brand colours only, contrast passes · approved fonts · sentence case · glossary terms · no "plan / tier / premium" · numbers formatted for the market · competitor claims sourced and first-hand · demo data only · legal footer · accessibility check · brand owner approval logged.

**New feature naming:** descriptive, lower case, one term, glossary updated, translated into the four launch languages, no trademark conflict.

**Partner asset request:** verified partner · lockup from library · no alteration · usage terms accepted · expiry when the partnership ends.
