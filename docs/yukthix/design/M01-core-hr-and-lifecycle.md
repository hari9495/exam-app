## 3. Design

### 3.1 Employee record
- **Sections** (T3 record workspace, UI brief):
  - **Overview** (photo, name, role, location, manager, status badges e.g. "On probation · ends 1 Apr", "Serving notice · last day 19 Oct");
  - **Job & assignment** (timeline, P06);
  - **Personal** (Personal class);
  - **Identity & bank** (Confidential/Special, masked);
  - **Family & nominees** (PF/gratuity/insurance nominations with shares);
  - **Emergency contacts**;
  - **Documents** (P05 checklist);
  - **Compensation** (link, M03);
  - **Leave & attendance** (summaries);
  - **Performance** (link);
  - **Assets**;
  - **Activity**.
- **Custom fields:** generalise the exam app's `CustomFieldDefinition` to employee (and other HR entities). Each custom field has a type, validation, section, **sensitivity class** (P02) and whether employees can self-edit.
- **India identity fields:** PAN (format + name match via P10), Aadhaar (Verhoeff checksum, stored encrypted, masked last-4), UAN (12 digits), ESIC IP number, IFSC (format + bank/branch lookup), passport and driving licence with expiry (P05). Duplicate detection across employees (same PAN / bank account → warning).
- **Bank accounts** (Q4): one **salary account** as a dated fact (P06) + an optional **reimbursement account**; changes through P02 YX-SEC-13 (approval + notify old contact) + P10 penny-drop.
- **Self-service edits:** Personal fields directly; identity/bank/legal-name via "change with approval" (P03 §4.8).
- **Annual health check-up (OSH Code, C1):** where P07 `IN.OSH` prescribes it for the establishment (dated parameters: age, frequency, covered categories; currently workers aged **40+**, once a year, **free of cost**), a yearly job builds the **eligibility list** per establishment from DOB and worker category, opens a **scheduling task** per person (owner HR / admin; provider, date; the employee is notified through P04 with no health content) and keeps a **check-up record** (done on, provider, fitness outcome, report as a P05 document). Outcomes and reports are **Special class** (P02): the employee and the named occupational-health / HR role see them; the manager sees only "scheduled / done". The employer bears the cost; nothing is deducted from pay. YX-EMP-13.

### 3.2 Org chart & reporting
- The org chart is built from current assignments; it supports search, expand/collapse, export, and "view as on date" (P06).
- **Dotted-line managers** (Q5): optional secondary managers per assignment, for visibility, feedback and project oversight. They are **not** in approval chains unless a policy names them (P03 subject-field resolver).
- Manager changes: the P06 change "manager change" (single or bulk when a manager leaves: "reassign 6 reports to…").

### 3.3 Job changes (P06, confirmed)
- **One change action with types:** promotion, transfer (same entity), inter-entity transfer (P01 Q5 five options), re-designation, salary revision, manager change, employment-type change, reorg (bulk).
- Each type declares: the facts it can change, approval policy (P03), **letter** (P05), **impact preview** (holiday calendar, PT/LWF state, shift, leave policy, approvers, cost centre), and effective-date rules.
- **A promotion with a pay change** creates the compensation record in the same change (fixes U52); M03 computes arrears/segments.
- Bulk changes (annual increment, reorg) via the wizard (T5), one approval, per-person preview, letters in bulk (P05).
- **Further change types** (E6, E7): **contract extension** (moves `contract_end_on`), **conversion to permanent** (fixed-term / intern → permanent type; probation per the company's contract-end policy), **retirement extension** (moves the retirement date) and **absence start / return** (below); all dated P06 changes with approval and letter.
- **Long absence** (E3, D17): **sabbatical, long LWP, maternity, long-term disability** are an **absence status** on the employment, a dated fact (P06) with `expected_return_on`. The start creates the status; the return (or a return-date change) is a dated change that fires **return-to-work tasks** (journey engine: IT re-enable, manager check-in, re-induction). Effects on accrual, attendance exceptions, PF NCP days, ESI and gratuity service are defined in **M02 / M03** (reference, not repeated here). The company defines its **sabbatical policy** (eligibility e.g. min. service, max length, paid / unpaid / part-paid, bond) from a **starter template**. YX-EMP-06.
- **Deputation / secondment / client-site posting** (E8): an assignment may carry **host** fields: `host_entity_id` or `host_client` (free text / M10 client), `host_location_id`. The host location drives the **holiday calendar, PT state and geofence** for the period; the home entity stays the employer (payroll, PF, ESI). A **cross-entity recharge** flag (with % or amount) lists the person in a monthly recharge report for finance (no accounting here). Start / end of deputation are dated changes with impact preview.
- **Bulk restructure** (E8): a **bulk transfer wizard** for mergers, demergers and entity restructure: pick people (by entity / department / list), target entity and structure, apply the **YX-ORG-17 transfer options as defaults** (leave, service continuity, code, F&F, salary structure) with per-person override, one approval, bulk letters. **Entity close / merge** steps: all active employments moved or exited, open journeys / cases / approvals re-parented, payroll of the closing entity run to its last period and locked (P08), entity marked closed (history kept). YX-EMP-07.
- **Inter-entity transfer continuity checklist** (E9): every inter-entity transfer (P01 YX-ORG-17) opens a checklist on the change: **PF UAN transfer** (same UAN; Form 13 or EPFO auto-transfer tracked), **ESI IP number** carried (same IP), **Form 12B** auto-populated from the old employment's YTD into the new entity's tax workspace (M03), **declarations and proofs** copied, **loans / advances re-parented** to the new employment when the F&F option is "no F&F", **leave-ledger transfer entries** (out / in, per the leave option), **gratuity**: when service continues, one gratuity is paid at final exit; when paid at transfer, the exemption already used is carried so the lifetime cap is applied across both payouts (P07). YX-EMP-08.

### 3.4 Probation & confirmation (Q3)
- Probation length defaults per employment type / grade (settings, P01 scopes), set at joining; the probation end is an employment milestone (P06).
- **Review:** N days before the end (default 15), the manager gets a review form. In **wave 4** this is a simple built-in form (outcome + comments + optional rating); from **wave 5** it uses the M06 review engine with a probation template. Outcomes: **confirm** (confirmation letter), **extend** (by X months, max total per policy, letter), **terminate** (starts an exit case with type "probation termination").
- **No action** → reminders → escalation to HR at the end date; auto-confirmation only if the tenant enables it (Q3).
- Confirmation can change leave eligibility, notice period and benefits (dated facts).

### 3.5 Onboarding (wave 4; Q1, Q2)
- **Sources:** accepted ATS offer (P01 §4.5), direct entry by HR, bulk import (CSV/Excel with validation preview). The accepted-offer hand-off follows the entity's **M10 Q2 mode**: **automatic** (employee created in pre-boarding on acceptance), or **manual** ("Create employee" from HR's "Ready to onboard" queue, pre-filled from the offer). **Waves:** in wave 4 the existing exam-app offer hands off to pre-boarding (basic); wave 7 (M10) adds structured offers from M03 templates.
  - **By person type (M10 hand-off matrix, D3):** a **new person** follows the flow above; an **ex-employee rehire** opens a **new employment on the existing employee record** (`employments.rehire_of`, P01 YX-ORG-19), pre-filled from the old record, with HR choosing what continues (service, leave, gratuity, code, probation) in an impact preview; an **internal employee** is **not onboarded**: the hire is a P06 transfer / promotion on the existing record (§3.3), effective on a **release date agreed with the current manager**; a **contractor** converting is a P06 employment-type change. The matrix matches on the **P01 person** (`persons`, YX-ORG-27), not on name or email alone; ex-employees and alumni are flagged already at **application / screening** (YX-LC-29), and a **contract worker** converting to employee keeps their contract history on the same person (YX-LC-30). YX-LC-11.
  - **Rehire policy (E5, D17):** the company sets a **rehire policy** with every option, each a default changeable **per rehire** by HR (with reason, audited):

    | Option | Choices |
    |---|---|
    | Employee code | reuse old code / new code (YX-ORG-16) |
    | UAN / PF | reuse UAN (always, same person); PF membership: continue / transfer old balance (Form 13) / fresh |
    | Same-FY tax | combine YTD income and TDS **per PAN × TAN × FY** when rehired into the same entity in the same FY (one Form 16), or treat as previous employer (Form 12B) |
    | Service continuity | continuous / break counted / fresh (seniority, notice, long-service awards) |
    | Gratuity service | add prior service (if not paid out) / fresh |
    | Leave balance | carry forward / encashed at exit, start fresh |
    | Probation | skip if break < N months (default 6) / always / never |
    | Prior warnings / PIP | visible to new manager / HR only / hidden |

    **Starter template** (labelled, company edits): **fresh start, same code and identity** (same employee record, same code, same UAN, service / gratuity / leave fresh, probation per normal rules, prior warnings HR-only). The impact preview shows the chosen options before HR confirms. Mirrors P01 YX-ORG-19. YX-LC-18.
- **Fixed-term, intern, apprentice** (E6, D17): at joining, fixed-term / intern / apprentice employments carry `contract_end_on` (field on `employments`, added in P01). Apprentice and intern categories take their statutory treatment (PF / ESI / bonus / gratuity applicability, stipend, NAPS / NATS) from the **P07 statutory matrix per employment-type category**; M01 does not hard-code it.
- **Campus / long pre-boarding** (E18): pre-boarding may last **months** (campus hires): the pre-boarder is **not billed** (above); the journey supports **engagement touchpoints** (scheduled messages, content, events, manager intro) at offsets from the joining date; campus hires can be grouped into a **batch** with a common joining date (changing the batch date re-anchors every member, YX-LC-13); an **LOI** (letter of intent, P05) can precede the formal offer, with the offer / appointment letter issued later in the same pre-boarding record. YX-LC-24.
- **Pre-boarding portal** (candidate, mobile-first, OTP login; exam-app candidate-portal pattern). The checklist is **configurable per entity / department / role** (Q2); the items below are the default:
  - welcome page (company, manager, first-day info);
  - forms (personal, family & nominees, emergency, education, previous employment incl. previous-employer income for TDS / Form 12B);
  - **document uploads** (P05 types, camera);
  - **e-sign** of appointment letter and policies (P05 Q2);
  - bank details (auto-verified, P10 Q6);
  - tax-regime choice;
  - **statutory items** (E11, HR / payroll-owned tasks in the default onboarding template): **UAN** generation or link of the existing UAN, **ESIC IP registration** (where ESI applies), **Form 11** (PF declaration), **Form 2** (PF / EPS nomination, from `employee_nominations`). M03 pre-flight (YX-PAY-05) flags a missing UAN / IP number. YX-LC-22.

  The completion % is visible to HR.
- **Joining:** on the joining date (or when HR clicks "Mark joined"), the candidate becomes an employee: employment + first assignment + user account (+ SSO provisioning); pre-boarding data moves into the record.
- **Appointment letter for every worker (OSH Code, C1):** an appointment letter (P05 template, APX-F #6) is **mandatory for every employee** of every type (permanent, fixed-term, probationer, intern, apprentice, retired re-employed, rehire), whether or not the hire came through the ATS. Every onboarding template carries an **"Issue appointment letter"** task owned by HR, due by the joining date (or the P07 `IN.OSH` deadline where one is set); it completes only when the P05 letter is **issued** (acceptance / e-sign is tracked separately). A missing letter after the due date raises a **compliance alert** to HR and the compliance calendar (P07) and shows as a warning in M03 pre-flight. The task can't be removed from a template (law, D17 exception); only its owner and offset can change. At go-live, employees with no letter on file are listed for a bulk issue (P05). YX-LC-26.
- **Pre-boarding exit states (D3):** until joining, a pre-boarder can leave the flow without becoming an employee:

  | State | Trigger | Unwind |
  |---|---|---|
  | **Postponed** | HR changes the joining date (candidate or company request) | Nothing is cancelled; the journey **re-anchors**: every open task's due date moves with the new joining date (YX-LC-13); draft compensation, probation and assignment dates move too |
  | **Did not join** | HR marks "did not join" on / after the joining date | HR **cancels the pre-boarding record** (employee + employment + draft compensation + pre-boarding login), releases the M10 headcount-plan slot and **reopens the requisition**; the candidate is marked **reneged** in the ATS (YX-LC-12) |
  | **Reneged** | Candidate declines after acceptance (portal or HR records it) | Same unwind as "did not join"; reason captured |
  | **Withdrawn after acceptance** | Company withdraws the offer (manual action by HR, reason mandatory) | Same unwind; withdrawal letter (P05); never automatic (BGV discrepancies only flag, §3.5 BGV) |

  Cancelled records are kept (status "cancelled", audited, P08), never hard-deleted. **Pre-boarding people are not counted as active employees for billing** (metering starts at joining).
- **Checklists (journey engine):** templates per entity / department / role; tasks with **owner** (HR, IT, admin, manager, **new hire**), due **relative to joining (negative allowed, fixes U49)**, dependencies, SLA reminders (P04); progress ring on the record and an onboarding board (UI brief). The same engine serves offboarding.
- **BGV:** follows M10 Q5: a paid add-on with the chosen partner (P10), or manual BGV tracking without it; triggered **after offer acceptance by default** (before, per company), with consent; results as documents; outcome gates (e.g. "clear before confirmation"); discrepancies go to HR and **never auto-withdraw the offer**.
- **Assets:** issue tasks in the checklist create asset assignments (§3.6).

### 3.6 Asset register (spec §1.3.10, simple version)
- **Assets:** type, name, serial / tag, purchase date and cost, legal entity, location, status (in stock / assigned / in repair / retired / lost).
- **Assignments:** employee, issued on, condition, acknowledgement (e-sign/click, P05), returned on, return condition.
- **At exit:** the unreturned-asset list flows into clearance, and optional recovery amounts flow into F&F.
- Bulk import; reports (assets by person / location).
- Depreciation and accounting are out of scope (finance system).

### 3.7 Offboarding (wave 4)
- **Exit types:** resignation, termination (incl. probation), **retrenchment** (with the related **lay-off** and **closure** workflows, below), retirement (date from DOB and policy age), end of contract, absconding, death; each with its own flow and letters (death, absconding, retirement and end of contract below).
- **Company lifecycle policies (D17):** every policy in this section that varies between companies (death support, absconding timeline, retirement, contract end, leave during notice, open-case hold; and rehire §3.5, sabbatical §3.3) is a **company setting** (P01 scopes) seeded from a clearly labelled **starter template** that the company edits; YukthiX never hard-codes it. Only legal rules (P07) are enforced, and a company may be more generous where the law allows. YX-LC-25.
- **Resignation (Q6):**
  1. The employee submits in the app (reason, proposed last day).
  2. Manager (+ HR) accept via P03.
  3. The **notice period** comes from policy by grade/type (and confirmation status), giving the standard last working day.
  4. Early release or buy-out computes the shortfall.
  5. Withdrawal before acceptance, or after acceptance with approval.
  6. The employee badge shows "Serving notice".
- **Leave during notice** (E10, D17): leave applied during notice follows the company's **per-leave-type notice policy** (defined in **M02**): **allowed** / **LOP** / **extends LWD** / **blocked**. **Starter template:** allowed; earned leave **may be adjusted against a notice shortfall** if the policy says so. The exit case shows the effect: an "extends LWD" leave moves `approved_lwd` (dated, with notice to the employee), and an adjustment reduces the F&F **notice shortfall** line (explained). YX-LC-21.
- **Exit pre-checks** (E16): when an exit case opens (any type), the system checks for an **open POSH / disciplinary case** where the person is respondent (M08) and an **open PIP** (M06), and flags them to HR on the exit case (never to the manager for POSH). The company policy (starter template: hold both until the case closes, HR may release) decides whether **relieving / experience letters** and / or **F&F** are **held pending the case outcome**; a hold never delays statutory dues beyond the legal deadline (YX-LC-08). The case **continues post-exit** with alumni-scoped access (M08). YX-LC-23.
- **Clearance:** checklist with department sign-offs (manager handover, IT access revocation, admin/assets, finance advances and expenses, HR documents). Each owner signs off; open items block F&F finalisation (not exit itself).
  - **Statutory items** (E11, default offboarding template, payroll-owned): **EPFO date of exit** marked on the unified portal, **ESIC exit** (IP marked left), **gratuity Form L** (employer's notice of gratuity amount) **within 15 days** of it becoming payable (P07), **PF transfer / withdrawal claim attestation** (Form 13 / 19 / 10C as applicable). Due dates are relative to the LWD. YX-LC-22.
- **Exit interview:** structured survey (reasons, ratings, would-rejoin), plus interviewer notes. Feeds P09 attrition analytics. Confidential answers go only to HR.
- **Last working day:** access switches to alumni (P05 Q6); P02 implicit grants are removed; open approvals are reassigned (P03 YX-WF-06).
- **Rehire eligibility:** flag + reason (HR only), shown when the person reappears as a candidate (ATS duplicate match).
- **Regretted exit:** a `regretted` yes / no flag on the exit case, set by HR only (never shown to the leaver or manager); feeds P09 regretted-attrition (P09 Q1).
- **Backfill:** the manager or HR ticks **backfill requested** on the exit case; on acceptance M10 opens a **replacement requisition** linked to the exit (M10 Q1: it reuses the leaver's plan line, no new line consumed).
- **Exit deprovisioning (D6):** every module that holds something for a person registers a handler for `employment.exited` (and, where the action must happen while the person is still active, for the LWD-approaching trigger). The exit case shows a **Deprovisioning** panel with one row per handler (pending / done / failed + retry, owner for manual follow-ups). Timing: **At LWD** = during the last working day, while the person is still active (handover); **T+0** = when `employment.exited` fires at the end of the LWD; **T+N** = N days later (tenant setting).

  | Module | What | Action | Timing |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | P02 / auth | User login, implicit grants | Convert to alumni; end grants (exists, YX-LC-05) | T+0 |
  | M04 | Registered devices, push tokens, sessions, kiosk PIN | Revoke (YX-MOB-09 path); disable PIN | T+0 |
  | P10 | Biometric device enrolments | Remove from every device (P10 Q4 sync) | T+0 |
  | P10 | Face template | Delete (exists, YX-AI-06) | T+0 |
  | P10 | Calendar OAuth connection | Revoke | T+0 |
  | P11 | User-owned API keys, OAuth grants | Revoke | T+0 |
  | P03 | Delegations where the leaver is delegate or delegator | End | T+0 |
  | P03 | Pending approvals awaiting the leaver | Reassign (exists, YX-WF-06) | At LWD |
  | T04 / T05 | Proctor shifts after LWD, evaluator queues | Unassign; items return to the pool / shift owner | At LWD |
  | M07 | Open enrolments, session seats after LWD | Cancel; release seats (waitlist promoted) | At LWD |
  | M08 | Helpdesk tickets owned, queue membership | Reassign to queue lead; remove from queue | At LWD |
  | M08 | Case memberships, IC seat | Replace member; **flag HR if the IC falls below its legal composition** | T+0 |
  | M06 | Goals, in-cycle reviews, PIPs owned | Transfer per the M06 owner-transfer rule | At LWD |
  | M09 | Engage spaces | Remove (exists) | T+0 |
  | M10 | Requisitions owned | Reassign to the hiring manager's manager / HR | At LWD |
  | M10 | Headcount-plan line | Backfill requested → replacement requisition (T+0); otherwise the position is released back to the plan | T+N (default 30) |
  | M05 | Open claims, advances | Pull into F&F (exists, YX-LC-07) | T+0 until F&F approved |
  | Assets | Unreturned assets | Clearance + F&F recovery (exists, §3.6) | At LWD |

  YX-LC-14, YX-LC-15.

- **Death in service** (E1, E-Q1 = A): a dedicated, compassionate flow started by HR (date of death, certificate upload, P05):
  1. **Immediate access revoke:** every D6 deprovisioning handler runs at once (all timings treated as T+0); LWD = date of death.
  2. **No exit interview and no employee sign-offs:** clearance items owned by the employee are closed by HR; asset returns are requested from the family, never recovered from dues without HR decision.
  3. **F&F payee = nominees** per `employee_nominations` (scheme and `share_percent`, e.g. gratuity nominees for gratuity, PF nominees for PF-linked lines); if there is no valid nomination, the **legal heir(s)** on production of succession documents (succession certificate / legal-heir certificate, P05). Each payee's **bank account is verified** (penny-drop, P10) before payout; the F&F is split into one payment per payee.
  4. **Amounts:** final salary, **leave encashment to the nominee**, statutory bonus, **gratuity with the 5-year service condition waived** (P07), reimbursements; recoveries only as HR confirms.
  5. **Tax:** dues paid to heirs follow the P07 treatment (e.g. amounts accrued after death are **not salary** of the deceased and are not subject to salary TDS where applicable); TDS up to the date of death is finalised and **Form 16 is issued to the legal heir** (P05).
  6. **Statutory claims support:** a **forms checklist** for the family: **EDLI** (Form 5 IF), **EPS family / widow / children pension** (Form 10D), **PF** (Form 20), **ESI dependant benefit** (where covered), gratuity (Form J by the nominee; the company responds with Form L); the company's attestations are tasks on the exit case.
  7. **Letters** (P05) addressed to the **next of kin** (condolence, dues statement, claim guidance), never to the deceased.
  8. **Nominee access:** each nominee / legal heir gets a **limited alumni-style login** (OTP) to view and download the deceased's F&F statement, Form 16 and claim documents only.
  - **Company support policy** (optional, **starter template**, company edits): **ex-gratia** amount (fixed / × monthly salary), **salary continuation for N months** to the nominees, **insurance claim hand-off** (group term / medical, M11 later; until then a manual task), a named **compassionate contact person** for the family, and dependant support items (e.g. education assistance, job for dependant). Starter template ships every item **off / blank** with guidance text. YX-LC-16.
- **Absconding** (E4, E-Q3 = A): a company-configurable **absconding timeline** that starts automatically from unauthorised absence (attendance with no leave, M02) or manually by HR. **Starter template** (all day counts and steps editable, steps may be removed or added):

  | Day of unauthorised absence | Step |
  |---|---|
  | 3 | **Auto salary hold** (M03 hold, reason "absconding") + alert to manager and HR |
  | 7 | **Notice 1** (P05 letter: return / explain by date) by **email + registered post** (dispatch and AD details recorded) |
  | 14 | **Notice 2** (final notice) by email + registered post |
  | 21 | **Deemed abandonment**: exit case of type absconding; **LWD = last day present** |

  After deemed abandonment: **recovery-only F&F** (dues are computed, recoveries applied, any net payable held until the employee claims it, never forfeited), **rehire-ineligible flag** set (HR may clear), deprovisioning (D6). HR can **stop** the timeline at any step (the employee returned; the absence is regularised in M02, salary released) or **resume** it; every step is audited on the exit case. YX-LC-17.
- **Retirement** (E7, D17): the company **retirement policy** sets the **retirement age per employment type / grade** (starter template: 58, date = end of the month of reaching the age) and an **alert lead time** (starter: 6 months, then 3 and 1). The alert opens an exit case of type retirement with the computed LWD. **Extension** is a dated change (§3.3) moving the date. **Re-employment** after retirement is a new employment of a **fixed-term "retired re-employed"** type (P07: no EPS contribution after 58; no probation; `contract_end_on` set). The exit triggers the **gratuity payout** (P07) and a **PF final settlement checklist** (Form 19 / 10D pension, EPFO exit, E11). YX-LC-20.
- **End of fixed-term / internship / apprenticeship** (E6, D17): the company's **contract-end policy per employment type**: **auto-exit** on `contract_end_on` / **alert and extend** / **convert to permanent**, with **reminder days** before the end (starter template: alert HR and manager at 30 and 7 days; no auto-exit). Extension and conversion are dated change types (§3.3, P06); an unactioned contract under "auto-exit" opens an end-of-contract exit case with LWD = `contract_end_on`. YX-LC-19.
  - **Fixed-term gratuity (SS Code, C1):** a fixed-term employee with **1 year** or more of continuous service gets **pro-rata gratuity** on leaving (end of contract or any earlier exit) under P07 `IN.SS-CODE` (from 21 Nov 2025; service before that date counted per the P07 transition rule); the 5-year condition does not apply to fixed-term employment. The F&F gratuity line explains the pro-rata basis. YX-LC-28.
- **Retrenchment, lay-off and closure (IR Code, C1):** retrenchment is an employer-initiated exit type governed by P07 `IN.IR` (dated, from 21 Nov 2025):
  1. **Pre-checks:** the exit case records the reason and the selection basis (it shows the establishment's seniority list for the worker's category, so HR documents last-come-first-go or its reasons for departing from it) and the worker's continuous service (the statutory compensation needs at least 1 year).
  2. **Notice or pay in lieu:** written notice of the period set by `IN.IR` (currently one month; three months where government permission is needed), or **wages in lieu of notice** as an F&F earnings line; the notice to the appropriate government is a task on the case (P05 letter).
  3. **Retrenchment compensation:** **15 days' average pay for every completed year** of continuous service (P07 formula) as an F&F earnings line.
  4. **Worker Re-skilling Fund:** an employer contribution of **15 days' last-drawn wages** per retrenched worker, raised as a statutory payable with its due date in the compliance calendar (P07); it is not an F&F line (the fund pays the worker).
  5. **Government permission (300+ workers):** where the establishment's worker count (IR Code definition, average per working day over the preceding 12 months, from P01 / P09) is at or above the `IN.IR` threshold (currently **300**), the exit case can't be approved until a **permission request** (establishment, workers affected, reasons, applied on, authority, outcome, order document) is recorded as **granted** (or deemed granted per `IN.IR`); a refusal cancels the retrenchment.
  - **Lay-off** and **closure** use the same permission request at establishment level: a lay-off records the affected workers and dates (the days reach M02 / M03 as an employment status with no attendance exceptions, YX-AT-10 pattern, and lay-off compensation per `IN.IR`); a closure records its notice / permission and opens retrenchment-type exit cases for everyone affected in bulk (§3.3 wizard pattern), with compensation per `IN.IR`. YX-LC-27.

### 3.8 Full & final settlement (Q7)
An **F&F calculator** (fixes U57), run as an **off-cycle payroll run** for the leaver (M03 engine) with explained lines:

| Area | Lines (each with "how it's calculated") |
|---|---|
| Earnings | Salary for days worked in the final month (attendance + P08 exceptions resolved), **leave encashment** per leave-type formula (Leave decision 5a), statutory **bonus** (P07), **gratuity** (P07 eligibility & formula; **pro-rata for fixed-term after 1 year**, YX-LC-28), pending reimbursements, arrears, **notice pay in lieu** (company buy-out, Q6), **retrenchment compensation** and **wages in lieu of retrenchment notice** (YX-LC-27) |
| Recoveries | **Notice shortfall** (policy), **negative leave balance** (Leave decision 6), outstanding **expense advances** (M05 Q6, fixes U65), **salary advances / loans** (M03 Q4), unreturned **assets**, **training-bond pro-rata recovery** (M07 Q5, after HR confirms or waives), **offer joining / retention bonus clawback** (M10 Q3), other |
| Tax | TDS recomputed for the year's final income; Form 16 handled at year end (P05) |
| Result | Net payable / recoverable; approval (P03) → payment (P10 bank file / payout) → **F&F statement letter** (P05) |

- A **deadline countdown** follows the Code on Wages (P07 `IN.WAGES-CODE`, in force since 21 Nov 2025): wages due on exit are paid **within 2 working days** of the last working day for **every exit type** (resignation, termination, retrenchment, closure, retirement, end of contract, absconding, death), counted on the entity's working-day calendar. The due date (LWD + 2 working days) is set when the exit case is approved; other dues keep their own P07 deadlines (e.g. gratuity under `IN.SS-CODE`). Holds (YX-LC-23), open clearance items and missing payee details (death without a valid nomination, absconding) never move the due date: the countdown keeps running, the case records why payment is pending, and HR can pay the wage lines first in one F&F run and settle the rest in a second. Overdue F&Fs are flagged to HR/Payroll. YX-LC-08.
- A recalculation before payment is allowed; after payment, changes follow P06/P08 corrections.
