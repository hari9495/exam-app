## 1. Purpose & scope
Pay the right people the right amount, on time, with statutory deductions and filings correct and explainable. Every figure on a payslip can be traced to its inputs and rules (the "why this number" drawer).

Out of scope here:
- expense reimbursement claims (M05 Expenses; approved claims and advance-recovery instalments arrive as payroll inputs);
- performance-linked pay rules (M06 produces comp-review changes and the bonus-pool split, which become compensation changes and one-time pay).

## 2. What exists today (exam app, `origin/main`)
There is no payroll code. We reuse the following:

## 3. Concepts
- **Component:** an earning, deduction, employer contribution, reimbursement or informational line. It has flags: taxable, part of PF wage, ESI wage, PT wage, gratuity wage, bonus wage, part of the Code wage definition (50 % add-back, YX-PAY-47), prorated, and shown on the payslip.
- **Salary template:** an ordered set of components with formulas (a safe expression language: `basic`, `ctc`, `payable_days`, `if()`, `min()`, `max()`, `round()`, and statutory functions such as `pf_employee()`).
- **Compensation:** an employee's dated package (P06). It records the template, the annual CTC or the fixed amounts, and any overrides. **Pay basis (D4):** monthly CTC / fixed amounts (above), or an **hourly or daily rate** with OT and holiday multipliers — for M10 placements the rate and multipliers come from the placement's rate card. Rate-based pay reads the M02 timesheet hours feed (§B6) and each line carries the `placement_id` for margin reporting (YX-PAY-24).
- **Pay group:** legal entity × pay frequency × pay calendar (cut-off per P08).
- **Payroll run:** one pay group and one period. It moves through draft → calculated → in review → approved (period **locked**, P08 YX-LOCK-04) → paid → published. Inputs are frozen at the P08 cut-off / when processing starts. **Off-cycle runs** (F&F, bonus, arrears only, corrections) use the same engine.
- **Payslip:** an immutable result per employee per run. Lines hold the value, the formula, the inputs, the rule version and the segment (P06). Once a run is approved, corrections are made only in the next run. **Imported payslips (J3):** months paid before go-live are imported as as-paid lines per component (P15 YX-MIG-15) into locked periods, marked `source = import` with the import batch; they are never recalculated, and they are the as-paid base for YTD tax and for arrears across the go-live boundary (YX-PAY-52).
- **Supplementary filing (J20):** a PF or ESI filing that corrects a month already filed (supplementary / arrear ECR, ESI past-period contribution, or an excess-remittance adjustment / refund request), linked to the original filing, which stays unchanged (YX-PAY-51).
- **One-time pay:** a single-period earning or deduction — bonus, incentive, recovery, reimbursement or arrear. It is recurring when it has a start and an end, and the last partial month is paid (fixes C-D9 / B-D10). Named input sources: M02 attendance feed (§B6); M05 approved claims and advance-recovery instalments; M06 comp-review changes and bonus-pool split; M09 reward payouts (taxable per P07); M10 referral bonus (YX-ATS-12), joining bonus / clawback (M10 Q3) and **placement commission** (staffing, E17); M08 suspension subsistence allowance (YX-PAY-23); M01 support-policy **ex-gratia / salary continuation** on death in service (YX-PAY-27); loans and advances (Q4).
- **Maternity benefit (E2, E-Q2):** a component = **average daily wage of the preceding 3 calendar months × maternity days** (P07 formula), plus the **₹3,500 medical bonus** when the employer provides no pre / post-natal care. **ESIC-covered employees are excluded** (ESIC pays). A **company top-up** to full salary is an option, starter template off (D17) (YX-PAY-26).
- **Salary hold (E13):** a withhold carries a **reason code** — absconding, pending documents, disciplinary, bank failure, exit clearance, other; statutory still runs on accrued pay (YX-PAY-28).
- **Payment mode (E12, D17):** `payment_mode` per employee — **bank** (default); **cash / cheque** only if the company enables it, each with a reason, approval and audit (YX-PAY-31).
- **Court attachment (E14):** a deduction type for garnishment orders, applied after statutory deductions and within the legal total-deduction cap (YX-PAY-32).
- **Subsistence allowance:** while an employee is suspended pending inquiry (M08 Q5), the run pays a subsistence-allowance component at the P07 rate (state standing orders) instead of normal pay (YX-PAY-23).
- **Tax workspace:** the employee's financial year view — chosen regime, declarations, proofs, previous-employer income (Form 12B), other income, and a projection.
- **Consultant (A8):** a non-employee payee (professional, technical consultant or contractor) in the **consultant register** per legal entity: PAN, GSTIN (optional), bank account verified per P10 (penny-drop), default TDS section (194C / 194J(a) / 194J(b)), and any **lower / nil deduction certificate u/s 197** (validity, limit, rate). Not an employee: no payslip, PF, ESI, PT or LWF (YX-PAY-37).
- **Consultant invoice (A8):** a monthly invoice uploaded by the consultant through the **Consultant** limited login (P02 §4.7) or by HR; it carries invoice no., date, taxable value, GST split (CGST / SGST / IGST) and the TDS section; approved through P03, then TDS is computed and it is paid in the same bank file / payout as the month's run (YX-PAY-38, YX-TAX-16).
- **Statutory register (A9):** a register in the official format from P07 `IN.REGISTERS` valid on the period date for the location's state (labour-code unified registers, or a legacy state Shops & Establishments / Factories Act format while a state still requires it); generated monthly from payroll and attendance, frozen on the period lock and DSC-signed. The **inspection pack** bundles all registers for a period × location (× entity) (YX-PAY-39).
- **Earned wage access (EWA, B10):** an employee draws part of the salary **already earned this period** before payday. **Earned to date** = (the period's expected net pay ÷ period days) × days worked or payable so far per the M02 feed (approved leave counts, LOP / unapproved absence does not), minus EWA already drawn this period, minus deductions already due (loan EMIs, advance instalments, court orders). The company sets an **EWA policy** (D17; starter template off): eligibility (e.g. confirmed, not serving notice, no salary hold), **% of earned-to-date available** (starter 50 %), per-draw minimum / maximum, **draws per period** (starter 3), blackout days before payroll cut-off (starter 3), who pays the fee (employee / employer / split). **Funding:** **partner-funded** (starter; an EWA partner pays the employee and is repaid from payroll, so the **employer carries no credit risk**) or **employer-funded** (reuses the salary-advance type, Q4, no partner). Partner access and per-draw fees are a **third-party add-on** (D18); the fee is **disclosed in rupees before the employee confirms**. Each draw is **recovered automatically in the next payroll** as a deduction (partner-funded: remitted to the partner with the bank file / payout; YX-PAY-40/41).
- **Cost rate (B1):** a per-employee hourly cost used by M12 project costing = the employee's employer cost for the month (gross + employer contributions from the approved payslip, or from compensation for the current open month) ÷ the company's standard hours (starter: expected working hours from M02 for that month); Restricted (P02) — project managers see project totals, never an individual's rate (M12 YX-PRJ-08).
- **Country pack (C4):** everything country-specific in payroll — components and wage definitions, tax, social security, end-of-service benefits, statutory files and payslip mandatory fields — comes from a **P07 country pack** attached to the legal entity's country (India is the first pack, `IN.*`). The run engine, approvals, locks, payslip lines and explanations are country-neutral; a pay group belongs to one entity, so one run = one country and one currency. First additional packs: **UAE** (`AE.*`: WPS **SIF** salary file, **end-of-service gratuity** per UAE labour law, basic-salary-based leave salary, pension for UAE / GCC nationals via GPSSA where applicable), **GCC variants** (Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait: WPS / mudad-style salary files, end-of-service gratuity rules, social insurance such as GOSI where the pack defines it), and **US 1099 contractor reporting only** (`US.1099`: W-9 capture, 1099-NEC annual totals and file / PDF for US contractors paid through the consultant register; **no US W-2 payroll** initially). Each pack's rules and formats are dated P07 data verified by a content owner (**to verify** per new country; India's Labour Codes and Income-tax Act 2025 status is settled, P07 §4.7 / §4.8).
- **ESOP (C4):** an **equity plan** (ESOP / RSU / SAR, per legal entity: pool size, vesting templates, exercise window rules, company valuation / FMV history) with **grants** to employees (grant date, number, exercise price, vesting schedule — time-based with cliff, or milestone / performance-based, or mixed), **vesting** events, **exercise** requests (employee, number, payment of exercise price), perquisite tax at exercise (YX-TAX-18), **sale tracking** (shares sold, date, sale price — for the employee's own capital-gains records and Form 130 / 12BA completeness) and **leaver treatment** per the plan (unvested lapse; vested exercise window after exit, starter 90 days; good / bad leaver). A **cap-table export** (CSV / XLSX of grants, vested, exercised, outstanding) is included; a live sync with an equity-management partner is an optional third-party add-on (D18).
- **Special pay types (V1, V2, V6, V7):** **piece-rate** pay (output per worker / day / operation × a dated rate card per operation, topped up to the minimum wage, OT on piece-rate basis per P07); **incentive and commission plans** (production / attendance incentives; sales commission with quota, tiers, accelerators, clawback and employee statements); **tips / service-charge pools** (per outlet, distributed by points, per shift or by hours); **on-call / standby / call-out** pay (standby allowance per slot, call-out minimum hours; the roster slot type is M02's). All plans start from labelled starter templates (D17); P07 law is the floor.
- **Industrial-relations pay (J4):** **check-off** union dues deducted per worker authorisation and remitted per union; **wage / long-term settlements** with retro arrears by worker category; **lay-off compensation** for M02 lay-off days; **VRS** payouts taxed per P07. The union register and strike / lockout / lay-off statuses live in M01 / M02.
- **Actuarial census (J24):** a year-end (and on-demand) census export for gratuity and leave-liability valuation (Ind AS 19 / AS 15); the actuary's figures are booked back as the provision.

## 5. Rules

### Payroll (YX-PAY)

| ID | Rule |
|---|---|
| YX-PAY-01 | Formulas run in a sandboxed expression language with no code execution. A template is validated on save: dependency order, no cycles, and a test run on a sample employee. Editing a template creates a new version and never changes past payslips (fixes A-D2, A-D3). |
| YX-PAY-02 | Proration uses the component's formula when one is defined. Otherwise it uses amount × payable days ÷ period days, with the day basis set per company (calendar days, 30 or 26). Never prorate twice (fixes B-D3). |
| YX-PAY-03 | A payslip is split into P06 segments whenever compensation, template or statutory status changes inside the period. Each line shows its segment (fixes B-D1). |
| YX-PAY-04 | An employee can have only one regular payslip per period and pay group; overlapping runs are rejected (fixes B-D4, C-D6). |
| YX-PAY-05 | The **pre-run validation** runs before calculation. It checks for: missing or unverified bank details, missing / unverified / **inoperative PAN** (a **warning, not a block** — TDS then follows s.206AA, YX-TAX-10), **missing UAN or ESI IP number** for PF / ESI-eligible employees (E11), no compensation, attendance exceptions, negative net pay, pay below minimum wage or the national floor wage (P07, YX-PAY-22), the **Code wage-definition add-back** (YX-PAY-47), joiners and leavers, pending one-time pay, and **impossible-value checks (G9, 26 Sep 2026)**: LOP days greater than the period's calendar days, pay or LOP for days before the joining date or after the last working day, approved leave (M02) not reflected in the attendance feed, a net-pay change above the company's threshold (starter 20 %) with no input change since the last approved run, and the same bank account number on more than one employee. Other findings can be waived with a reason before calculation; **open attendance exceptions cannot be waived** and block run **approval** (P08 YX-LOCK-08) — only for Punch-mode groups where the P08 setting is **block** (default); where it is **warn**, they are shown as warnings to acknowledge. Assumed-present groups raise no attendance exceptions; their LOP comes from HR's manual input (M02 YX-AT-09, D1). |
| YX-PAY-06 | **Variance review:** every payslip is compared with the previous period. Changes above a threshold (default ±10 % net, or any new or removed component) must be acknowledged before approval. |
| YX-PAY-07 | Approval follows the run's approval chain (Q2), with maker ≠ checker (P02 Q6). Inputs are frozen at the P08 cut-off / when processing starts; approval moves the period to **locked** (P08 YX-LOCK-04). Bank files, payslip publication and journals are only available after approval. |
| YX-PAY-08 | A run is never deleted once it has been calculated. Cancelling marks it void, keeps its payslips as void, and is audited (fixes C-D5). |
| YX-PAY-09 | A withheld payslip is released per employee per run. Releasing it affects only that run (fixes C-D2), and the action is idempotent (fixes C-D3). |
| YX-PAY-10 | Year-to-date figures only count payslips of the same employment in that FY whose period ends on or before the current one (fixes B-D5, B-D6). Tax YTD, TDS and Form 130 are combined across employments per PAN × TAN × FY (YX-TAX-13). |
| YX-PAY-11 | Payroll recoveries (loan EMIs, expense-advance instalments from M05, other recoveries) are deducted in the order set by their schedules. If net pay would fall below the protected minimum (company setting), the recovery is deferred rather than creating negative pay. Outstanding balances go to F&F. |
| YX-PAY-12 | Every payslip line stores its explanation (formula, inputs, rule version). The payslip PDF and the "why this number" drawer are rendered from it. |
| YX-PAY-13 | Bank files keep account numbers and IFSC codes as text (leading zeros preserved), include only approved, unwithheld, verified-account payslips, and are generated at most once per run unless regenerated with a reason (fixes D-D10, C-D4). |
| YX-PAY-14 | Payment status (paid / failed / returned) is recorded per payslip. Failed payments re-enter the next bank file or an off-cycle payment. On a return the employee is notified in the app (+ SMS, no amounts) to correct the bank details; the corrected account may skip the P02 §4.5 cooling period only after checker approval **and** a successful penny-drop; meanwhile payroll may pay to the employee's verified reimbursement account (D11). |
| YX-PAY-15 | Gratuity is provisioned monthly (last drawn gratuity wage × 15/26 × service years, capped at the statutory ceiling). Payment follows the law: 5 years of eligibility (**fixed-term employees: pro-rata after 1 year** of continuous service, Code on Social Security, in force 21 Nov 2025, P07 `IN.SS-CODE`), a final year of 6 months or more rounds up, cap from P07 (fixes D-D5); from 21 Nov 2025 the gratuity wage is the Code wage with the 50 % add-back (YX-PAY-47). |
| YX-PAY-16 | Statutory bonus is computed per the Payment of Bonus Act rules in P07 (eligibility wage, calculation ceiling, 8.33–20 %). The payment method follows Q8. |
| YX-PAY-23 | While an employee is suspended pending inquiry (M08 Q5), the run pays a subsistence-allowance component at the P07 rate (state standing orders) instead of normal pay, until the suspension ends. |
| YX-PAY-24 | A compensation's pay basis is monthly CTC, fixed amounts, or an **hourly / daily rate** with OT and holiday multipliers (from the M10 rate card for placements). Rate-based pay = approved hours (or days) from the M02 timesheet hours feed × rate × the category multiplier; unapproved hours are never paid. Each rate-based payslip line carries its `placement_id` for margin reporting (D4). |

## 6. Flows
1. **Setup wizard (per entity):**
   1. Registrations, incl. deductor / responsible-person details; each statute switched on runs a completeness check (YX-PAY-36).
   2. Pay group and cut-off.
   3. Template choice (India standard CTC template shipped).
   4. Bank format.
   5. Accounting mapping.
   6. Opening balances for employees who joined partway through the year, and year-to-date migration (Form 12B / imported YTD).
2. **Compensation:** new or revised through a P03 request. Salary revision letters come from P05. The entry mode follows Q1.
3. **Monthly run:**
   1. Readiness screen: attendance mode per group (Punch block / Punch warn / Assumed present / Timesheet, D1); inputs from M02 (attendance feed; timesheet hours feed for rate-based pay, D4), **manual LOP / payable-days input** for Assumed-present groups (HR enters LOP days per employee for the period, or bulk upload by CSV / XLSX with row validation, each with a reason), M05 (approved claims, advance-recovery instalments), M06 (comp-review changes, bonus-pool split), M09 (reward payouts), M10 (referral bonus, joining bonus / clawback), M08 (subsistence allowance), one-time pay, loans, joiners and leavers, validations.
   2. Calculate (fan-out job) with per-employee progress; failures name employee, record and field (YX-PAY-34).
   3. Review variance and exceptions.
   4. Submit for approval.
   5. Approve (locks the period, P08 YX-LOCK-04; blocked while attendance exceptions are open, where the P08 setting is block).
   6. Bank file or payout; cash / cheque payslips go to the disbursement register (YX-PAY-31); held payslips stay out until released (YX-PAY-28).
   7. Record payment status. **Payment failure sub-flow (D11, YX-PAY-14):**
      1. A failed / returned payment is marked with the bank's reason.
      2. The employee is notified in the app (+ SMS without amounts) to correct the bank details.
      3. **Expedited bank change** (P02 §4.5 exception): checker approval + a successful penny-drop waive the cooling period.
      4. Meanwhile, if the employee has a verified reimbursement account, payroll may pay there (with a reason, audited).
      5. The payslip is re-included in the next bank file or paid in an off-cycle payment.
   8. Publish payslips (in-app, with a notification).
   9. Journal export.
   10. Statutory checklist.
4. **Statutory month:** after the run is paid, the checklist shows PF ECR, ESI, PT, LWF, TDS challans (salary and consultant sections) and the register sign-off with due dates from the compliance calendar (P07 `calendar_rules` via the P04 scheduler SCH-07, `compliance.due.upcoming`; M03 raises no due dates). Each item moves generate → download → mark uploaded with acknowledgement / challan no. → filed lock (P08). With the **compliance partner** (Q7(c)), PF / ESI / PT / LWF items move generate → transmit to partner → filed by partner → acknowledgement synced back (or rejected, with the reason) → filed lock. **Correcting a filed month (J20):** the correction (arrears, missed employee, wrong wage, excess) creates a supplementary filing under the original month's card → generate supplementary ECR / ESI past-period contribution (or adjustment / refund request) → interest / damages lines shown → upload or partner → acknowledgement → filed lock (YX-PAY-51).
5. **Quarterly TDS:** challans (pre-filled from approved runs and paid consultant invoices, one per section, with interest / fee, YX-TAX-14) → Form 138 and **Form 140** drafts (24Q / 26Q for quarters up to 31 Mar 2026, YX-TAX-19) (incl. prior-month deductee rows for mid-year starters, YX-TAX-15) → deposit-vs-deduction reconciliation (YX-PAY-36) → validate → file (Q6) → acknowledgement. **Self-file:** FVU validation in our worker → HR uploads on the portal → acknowledgement recorded → Part A imported from TRACES. **Partner:** transmitted → filed by partner → acknowledgement and Part A fetched automatically. After Form 140 is filed, **Form 131** per consultant is issued, DSC-signed and published in the Consultant login (YX-TAX-17). **Correction return (J21):** correction Form 138 / 140 filed and accepted → corrected Part A fetched → revised Form 130 / 131 generated, earlier certificate superseded, notice with reason and correction statement to each affected person (YX-TAX-20).
6. **Year end:**
   - Proof window.
   - Verification.
   - Final TDS true-up.
   - Form 130 (Form 16 for FY 2025-26 and earlier, YX-TAX-19): Part A per Q6; Part B generated by us; signed with the company DSC; published to employees and alumni (P05).
   - Bonus (Q8).
   - Gratuity provision statement.
7. **Off-cycle runs:** F&F (M01), bonus, arrears only, correction. Same engine, same approval, their own bank file. **F&F** runs are created automatically on the approved LWD with a **2-working-day** payment deadline for every exit type (YX-PAY-48); a retrenchment adds compensation, notice-pay and re-skilling fund lines (YX-PAY-49). **F&F on death in service** pays nominees by share or the legal heir (YX-PAY-27).
8. **Consultant payouts (A8):**
   1. HR adds the consultant to the register (PAN, GSTIN, bank verified per P10, TDS section, any s.197 certificate) and optionally invites them to the Consultant login (P02 §4.7, OTP).
   2. The consultant (or HR) uploads the monthly invoice with its GST details.
   3. Approval through P03 (YX-PAY-38).
   4. TDS computed per section, threshold, PAN status and certificate (YX-TAX-16), shown line by line.
   5. Paid in the month's bank file / payout (or off-cycle); payment advice published; journal with GST input credit and TDS payable (P10 Q7).
   6. TDS deposited on the section challan → Form 140 quarterly → Form 131 (YX-TAX-17).
9. **Registers (A9):** when the period locks (P08), the registers P07 `IN.REGISTERS` requires per location are generated, frozen and DSC-signed (YX-PAY-39); the statutory hub shows a monthly **register sign-off** item from the compliance calendar; the inspection pack is available per period × location (× entity); a correction creates a new signed version with a reason.
10. **Earned wage access (B10, wave 5):**
    1. The employee opens Pay › Get paid early (M04): available amount, fee, recovery date.
    2. Enters an amount, sees the fee disclosure, confirms (re-authentication, M04 Q8).
    3. Partner-funded: the request goes to the EWA partner (P10 connector), which pays the verified salary account and confirms; employer-funded: an auto-approved salary advance (Q4) is paid in the next bank file / payout.
    4. The draw appears as a pending recovery on the employee's payroll readiness row; the next run deducts it (YX-PAY-41) and the partner is paid with the bank file; reconciliation per draw.
11. **Country packs (C4, wave 6):** entity setup picks the country → the pack's setup cards (registrations, WPS agent / establishment ids, gratuity basis, currency) → completeness check → pay groups in that currency → monthly run → pack files (e.g. UAE SIF) → EOSB provision; US: consultant register W-9 → year-end 1099-NEC.
12. **ESOP (C4, wave 6):** HR / finance sets up the plan and valuation → grants (P03 approval, grant letter via P05 e-sign) → vesting events (automatic by date; milestones confirmed by an approver) → employee requests exercise in the app (units, price payable, perquisite and TDS preview) → approval → exercise price received → perquisite in the month's run (or deferred, YX-TAX-18) → allotment recorded → optional sale tracking → cap-table export.

**Events emitted:** `payroll.run.calculated`, `.approved`, `.paid`, `.voided`, `payslip.published`, `payment.failed`, `recovery.deferred`, `tax.proof.verified`, `statutory.filing.filed` / `.rejected`, `loan.closed` (full list and consumers: [Appendix B §2.12](APX-B-events.md)). M03 does **not** emit `statutory.filing.due` (retired): due dates come only from the P04 scheduler rule SCH-07 over the P07 compliance calendar (APX-B §3 #1); `statutory.filing.filed` marks the calendar item done. Consumers: P04, P09 metrics, M05, M01, the accounting webhook.
