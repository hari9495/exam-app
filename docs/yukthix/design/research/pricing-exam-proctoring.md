Research date: 2026-09-27. Budget: 20 web calls, all used. Rows for Respondus, Proctorio, Honorlock and ProctorU come from `research-proctoring.md` §4 and were not re-fetched.
Confidence: **H** = the vendor's own pricing page, fetched. **M** = an institution's page or a search snippet quoting the vendor. **L** = a third-party aggregator or an estimate.
FX assumption used only for the INR to USD conversions: **₹88 = $1**. This rate is an assumption. Recheck it before using the figures.

## 4. Pricing patterns

1. **Per exam, higher-ed US (Proctorio, Honorlock, Examity, Proctortrack, ProctorU).** AI-only costs about $3.75–9 per exam. With human review it is $5.75–12.50. Live proctoring is $11–33 and is **priced by duration**.
2. **Per seat per year (Respondus, Honorlock site licence, Proctorio unlimited).** This runs about $2–25 per student per year. It is cheaper when each student sits many exams.
3. **Credit-based pricing (India and SMB: AutoProctor, SpeedExam, Eklavvya, ClassMarker).** One credit is one attempt. Stronger proctoring **uses more credits per attempt**: AutoProctor charges +4 to +15 credits and ClassMarker charges 10×.
4. **Results per month (Testportal, SpeedExam caps).** Cheap per result, but these products have light or no webcam proctoring. Monthly caps also block one-day spikes such as a campus drive.
5. **Quote-only enterprise (Mettl, Talview, Wheebox, ExamSoft, OnVUE, OctoProctor, Constructor, Examly, CodeTantra).** These carry volume minimums, and Talview's is ~150–200 candidates per year.
6. **Free tiers are tiny.** They range from 10 to 25 attempts: AutoProctor 10, SpeedExam 25/mo, Eklavvya 25. Respondus is the exception with 200 seats bundled.
7. **Nobody publicly bundles AI + live + secure client + evaluation into one flat per-attempt price.** YukthiX's "$1 per started attempt" has no direct published comparator.

## 5. Effective cost per attempt

Assumptions:
- An "attempt" is one candidate starting one exam.
- Exams are 60 minutes or less.
- For per-seat pricing, each seat takes **one** attempt. This is the worst case for that model.
- INR is converted at ₹88 = $1.
- The 5,000-candidate campus drive runs inside **one month**.
- Only published prices are used. A dash means there is no public price.

### 5c. Reading the numbers
- **Versus US higher-ed proctoring,** YukthiX's $1 is **3.75–17× cheaper** per attempt. The range runs from Examity Auto Std ($3.75) to ProctorU live ($16.80), and YukthiX also bundles live proctoring and evaluation.
- **Versus Indian and SMB credit tools,** basic AI-only proctoring is **cheaper than $1**: AutoProctor costs $0.10–0.16 and ClassMarker camera attempts cost $0.80. Neither offers live proctoring, a secure client or evaluation. Once AutoProctor's recording and ID add-ons are included, it costs **$2.45 per attempt with no live proctoring**, so YukthiX's $1 is still less than half.
- **Seat licences (Respondus) reach $2.35–2.75 per seat at 5k–10k seats.** They get cheaper only when students sit multiple exams. Pitch per-attempt pricing to schools with low exam frequency.
- **Campus drives (5,000 in one month) break most SMB caps** (SpeedExam, Testportal, Testmoz, ClassMarker) and push buyers to quotes. A flat $1 with no monthly cap is a real selling point.
- **Margin risk.** Competitors charge $11–16.80 for a live proctored hour. Including live proctoring in $1 only works at a high candidate-to-proctor ratio, or if live is AI-triggered (a human pops in on flags) rather than continuous. Define "live" carefully in the terms.

## 6. Gaps and caveats
- The following have no published prices: Mettl, Wheebox, ExamSoft, OnVUE, Excelsoft, Think Exam, Examly, CodeTantra, OctoProctor, Constructor and Eklavvya paid tiers. Getting these needs sales quotes or RFP data.
- ClassMarker dollar amounts come from a search snippet (M). Their pricing page fetch showed the credits but not the dollar amounts.
- SpeedExam's proctor-credit price is unpublished, so its costs above are a floor.
- Proctorio and Honorlock ranges are M/L (institution pages and aggregators). The Examity rates are institution rate cards found by search, not Examity's own page.
- Page failures (skipped): classmarker.com/pricing (404), speedexam.net/pricing.aspx (404), testportal.com/en/pricing (404), and proctoredu.com/pricing, which redirects to octoproctor.com and shows no prices.
