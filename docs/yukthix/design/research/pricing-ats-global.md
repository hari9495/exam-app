## 4. Pricing patterns

1. **Four billing units.**
   - Per recruiter seat: Manatal, Zoho, LinkedIn.
   - Flat per plan: Breezy, JazzHR.
   - Per job slot: Homerun, Teamtailor.
   - By **total company headcount**: Workable, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Pinpoint, Recruitee, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS, Jobvite, Gem, BambooHR.

   Headcount pricing is the dominant model in mid-market and enterprise: the bill grows with employees, not with recruiting effort.
2. **Seat-based vendors still cap jobs.** Manatal allows 15 jobs on Professional. Zoho allows 1, 10 or 20 active jobs per licence. JazzHR Hero allows 3 jobs, and Breezy's free plan allows 1.
3. **Hiring managers are usually free or unlimited** (Manatal, Breezy, Pinpoint). YukthiX's free hiring managers are table stakes, not a differentiator.
4. **Feature gating drives upgrades.** Common gates:
   - Scorecards and e-sign: Breezy Growth.
   - Offers and nurture campaigns: Breezy Business.
   - SSO, API and LLM: Manatal Ent Plus, Recruitee Optimize, Ashby add-on.
   - Texting and video: Workable add-ons, iCIMS modules.
5. **AI is increasingly metered by credits:** Workable at $0.095–0.12 per credit, Breezy at $30 per 100k credits, Recruitee with 100 screening credits a month.
6. **Contract traps.**
   - Minimum terms: 12 months (Recruitee, Teamtailor, Homerun) and 36 months (iCIMS).
   - Auto-renew with 3–15% escalators: Greenhouse, iCIMS, Teamtailor, Jobvite.
   - Implementation fees of $1k–100k+ in mid-market and enterprise.
   - Price cliffs by headcount band: Workable's price jumps at 21 employees.
7. **Only SMB tools publish prices:** Manatal, Zoho, Breezy, Homerun, Workable (1–20 band only), JazzHR and BambooHR. Every mid-market or enterprise vendor is quote-only.

## 6. Gaps and caveats
- Recruitee, Teamtailor, Pinpoint, Lever, Greenhouse, Ashby Plus, SmartRecruiters, iCIMS and Jobvite figures are third-party estimates (Vendr, pin.com, prepzo, leonstaff), not rate cards.
- Teamtailor's pricing unit conflicts between sources: job slots (pin.com) vs €99/seat (prior).
- The Pinpoint band table from prepzo doesn't state whether figures are monthly or annual.
- Zoho's $25/$50 are aggregator figures; the vendor page didn't render prices. The Professional tier wasn't shown on the page.
- JazzHR Plus/Pro differ between sources ($3,228 vs $3,480; $5,040 vs $5,508).
- Workable prices above 20 employees are quote-only.
- No data found for Greenhouse or Ashby list prices at exactly 200 or 1,000 employees; ranges are used.
