# Global HRMS / HRIS / Payroll — Customer Complaints, Gaps, Pricing (research for YukthiX)

Date: 2026-09-26. Method: 30 web searches + ~60 page fetches (Capterra, Trustpilot, Hacker News, Blind, vendor pricing pages, analyst/aggregator reviews). G2 and TrustRadius block automated fetches (HTTP 403), so G2/TrustRadius data below is second-hand via search snippets or aggregators and is marked **[2nd-hand]**. Quotes are verbatim from the fetched page unless marked *(paraphrase)*. Frequency labels: **High** = named as a top theme by the review site's aggregate or appears repeatedly across several sources; **Med** = appears in multiple reviews; **Low** = 1–2 reviews found. Reviewer type in brackets.

Legend for reviewer type: HR = HR admin/HR manager; PAY = payroll manager/bookkeeper; EXEC = owner/CEO/COO/CFO; EMP = end-user employee; CTR = contractor paid via platform; IT = IT/sysadmin/dev.

### 1.22 Freshteam (discontinued) — why
Sources: https://www.peoplematters.in/news/business/freshworks-to-end-freshteam-hr-product-stop-renewals-from-march-2026-47939 ; https://www.lenavio.com/blog/freshteam-dead-alternative ; https://blog.hrpartner.io/freshteam-alternative/
- Timeline: announced 7 Jan 2026; renewals stop 7 Mar 2026; access ends ~Apr 2027. ~9,000 customer companies (Lenavio figure; not confirmed by Freshworks).
- Stated reason: consolidate into "Freshservice for Business Teams"; "no material business impact" (Freshworks).
- Underlying: moved under Freshservice in 2022, engineering reassigned, "stopped receiving meaningful updates", labelled "Legacy" on Gartner. Analyst view: loss-leader to cross-sell Freshdesk/Freshsales that did not convert.
- What users liked (the gap now open): cheap, simple ATS + core HR + onboarding + time-off in one; migrants want the same at transparent per-employee pricing with no platform fee. Lesson for YukthiX: an HR side-product inside a non-HR company dies; cheap+simple ATS+HRIS demand exists.
