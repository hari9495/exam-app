# Pricing: India ATS, Job Boards and Staffing/Agency Tools

Prepared for YukthiX (ATS at **$1 ≈ ₹96 per recruiter seat per month**; hiring managers free; staffing desk, vendor portal, client portal and GST invoicing included). Date: 2026-09-27.
FX used throughout: **$1 = ₹96**. GST (18%) is **excluded** everywhere unless noted.
Confidence: **H** = vendor page read directly; **M** = vendor-adjacent, search snippet, or reseller; **L** = third-party tracker or blog, or a conflicting value.
Budget note: 20 web calls used. Naukri, WorkIndia and Instahyre pricing pages returned 403 and Zoho's IN pricing URL returned 404, so those figures come from secondary sources. iimjobs/hirist and Skillate publish no prices. Nothing below is invented; "n/p" means not public.

## 4. Pricing patterns

1. **Job boards sell reach and data, not software.** Naukri, foundit, Apna, WorkIndia, Instahyre and Indeed charge per post (₹1,000–₹1,650), per credit bundle (Apna ₹1,949 for 3 jobs), per CV view (Naukri ₹4,000 per 100 = **₹40/CV**), or per click (Indeed). Full database seats (Resdex) are quote-only and run into lakhs. None of them sells a real ATS publicly: Naukri RMS is n/p, and Apna's "ATS integration" is enterprise-only.
2. **India HRMS vendors sell the ATS as a per-recruiter add-on at ₹999–₹4,000/mo** (≈ $10–42). It sits on top of a mandatory HRMS base, and none of them includes client or vendor portals or staffing features.
3. **Global agency ATS/CRMs charge $85–$199 per seat** (≈ ₹8,000–₹19,000/mo), usually annual. Client portals appear at mid tiers (Crelate Business, Zoho Enterprise). Vendor portals are rare and separately priced (Zoho add-on, Ceipal VMS bundle). Data credits are the main upsell (Loxo, Recruit CRM).
4. **Low-cost outliers**: Manatal at $15–55 (unlimited free hiring managers, the closest model to YukthiX), Ceipal ATS at ~$25 with a 5-seat minimum, and Zoho Recruit at ₹1,250–₹3,750.
5. **Nobody bundles GST invoicing** (e-invoice/IRN) with timesheets and portals. Agencies stitch the ATS to Zoho Invoice or Tally, which confirms the finding in research-opensource-ats.md.
6. **YukthiX at ₹96/seat** is about 10× below sumHR (₹999), about 13–40× below Zoho Recruit, about 15× below Manatal Professional (₹1,440), and about 85–200× below agency ATSs. It sits near the price of job-board credits, not of ATS seats. So the price is a positioning statement. Room to go higher: even ₹499/seat undercuts every per-seat ATS listed here.

## 5. Annual cost scenarios (ex-GST, $1 = ₹96)

### (a) Indian company: 2 recruiters, 10 jobs a year

ATS cost = seats × price × 12. Job-board spend is separate and the same whichever ATS is used. Illustration: 10 Naukri Hot Vacancies = 3 × ₹4,950 + 1 × ₹1,650 = **₹16,500**, versus 10 × ₹1,650 = ₹16,500 (the 3-pack gives no discount). Cheaper alternatives: Apna 6-month bundle (13 credits) **₹7,099**, or Naukri standard posts at ₹0.

|---|---|---|---|
| **YukthiX** | 2 × ₹96 × 12 | **₹2,304** | ₹18,804 |
| Zoho Recruit Free | 1 active job/licence → only 2 jobs live at once | ₹0 | ₹16,500 (may not fit) |
| Manatal Professional | 2 × $15 × 12 = $360 × 96 | ₹34,560 | ₹51,060 |
