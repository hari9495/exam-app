## Checked and covered (no gap)

- **Maternity Benefit Act and crèche at 50+:** P07 `IN.LEAVE`, M02 YX-LV-10, M03 YX-PAY-26 (ESIC switch, top-up).
- **POSH Act:** IC validity, 90-day clock, annual report to the District Officer (M08 YX-POSH-01..06, APX-F #48, SCH-07).
- **Apprentices Act:** statutory matrix, NAPS / NATS stipend (P07 `IN.APPRENTICE`, M01 YX-LC-19).
- **State S&E variations:** P07 registers with legacy S&E / Factories formats dated per state (YX-STAT-16), OT limits (`IN.FACTORIES`), PT / LWF for all states.
- **Unified registers under the codes:** P07 `IN.REGISTERS` A9-Q2 (formats need re-verifying under R1).
- **EPS higher pension:** P07 EPF row ("EPS higher-pension option"). UAN generation / link: M01 YX-LC-22.
- **ESIC:** rates, ceiling, continuation, PwD ceiling (P07; M03 test at ₹21,000).
- **Payment of Wages / Code on Wages deduction cap and garnishment:** M03 YX-PAY-32.
- **CLRA / OSH contract-labour thresholds:** M13, P07 `IN.CLRA`.
- **DPDP core:** DSAR incl. nomination, consent artefacts, consent-manager adapter, DPO, DPIA / RoPA, breach workflow, minors (M10 YX-ATS-19), CERT-In 6 h (P02 §6, P12 Q6).
- **GDPR / UK GDPR:** processor DPA (Art. 28), Art. 9 biometric consent, Art. 33 72 h (APX-G G-03/G-08).
- **Illinois BIPA:** written release and public retention schedule (APX-G G-08, G-13).
- **EU AI Act prohibited emotion recognition:** never built (P10 YX-AI-10).
- **UAE WPS SIF and end-of-service gratuity:** M03 YX-PAY-44.
- **Companies-Act-style whistleblower / vigil mechanism:** M08 Q8, audit-committee routing.
- **GST e-invoicing IRN / QR on invoices:** APX-F #83–84, M10 Q7.
