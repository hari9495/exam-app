## 2. Features by plan (what is gated where)

- **Proctoring is usually gated to the top tier or an add-on.** CoderPad has webcam proctoring only on Enterprise. Criteria's base plan has non-AI proctoring, and AI proctoring needs the "AI" upgrade. Testlify is the exception: it includes tab/mouse tracking, recording, face detection and AI-assist detection on the $139 Standard plan, but ID verification is Custom-only.
- **SSO and ATS integrations are gated.** Codility offers them on Custom only, and CoderPad on Enterprise only. Testlify charges $2,388/yr for ATS, and SSO comes only with Custom. Spark Hire sells SSO and API as add-ons. Criteria and Willo include SSO on all plans.
- **Library size scales with the tier:** HackerRank 2k to 4k questions, CoderPad 400 to 1,400 to 4,000.
- **Custom questions are capped on low tiers:** CoderPad allows 10 on Starter and 20 on Team.
- **Seats:** some tiers cap users (HackerRank Starter 1, Codility 1 or 3, HackerEarth 2 or 5 admins, Spark Hire monthly 5). Others allow unlimited users (CoderPad, Willo Enterprise, HackerRank Pro).
- **White-label:** Testlify $2,388/yr add-on (included in Custom). Spark Hire charges for extra brands (price not listed). Willo includes custom branding.

## 4. Pricing patterns

1. **Annual bundle plus overage** is the dominant model for coding tests: HackerRank, CodeSignal, CoderPad, Testlify. List overage settles around **$20–30 per attempt**, and that is the real marginal price.
2. **Unused bundle credits expire** at the end of the cycle (Adaface, Testlify, TestGorilla, CodeSignal). Only Adaface refunds an unstarted invite. TestGorilla charges on start, which is the same trigger YukthiX uses.
3. **Annual-only lock-in** is common: TestGorilla, Adaface, Codility Starter, Spark Hire ATS, Willo Enterprise. Spark Hire states it gives no refunds.
4. **Video interview tools price per seat, role or subscription with unlimited interviews** (Spark Hire, Willo), so their marginal cost per candidate goes to near zero at volume.
5. **Psychometrics and enterprise tools are quote-only:** Criteria, SHL, Mettl, iMocha, Xobin, Glider, Talview, HireVue, Vervoe, Intervue, BarRaiser. Third-party estimates put them at $5k–50k/yr.
6. **Human-led interviews** (Karat, interviewing.io, BarRaiser) cost **$179–450 per interview**, a different order of magnitude.
7. **Upsells are proctoring, SSO, ATS and white-label.** Competitors' entry prices do not include "everything".

Takeaways:
- For proctored coding and skills tests, public list prices are **$12–30 per attempt at 100/yr** and **$17–24 at 1,000/yr**.
- The only public plans that fall below $10 are Adaface's large prepaid bundles ($5.00–5.53, with annual expiry and no proctoring detail) and Coderbyte's $10 pack.
- YukthiX at $1 is roughly **10–25× below every public list price** for coding and skills tests. It is also below the per-candidate cost of async video tools until about 3,000–4,000 attempts/yr.
- The unlimited-video subscriptions (Spark Hire, Willo) undercut $1 only at high volume, and they are video interviews only.
