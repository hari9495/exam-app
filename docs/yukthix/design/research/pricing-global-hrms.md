# Global HRMS / HRIS / Payroll / EOR / Performance — Pricing & Features Catalog (for YukthiX)

Researched 2026-09-27. Extends and checks §4 of `research-global-hrms.md`.
**Confidence:** **H** = vendor pricing page fetched today · **M** = aggregator or buyer data (Vendr, OutSail, Capterra/G2 snippets, tinyteam, costbench and similar) · **L** = blog or single-source estimate, or a price the vendor no longer publishes.
Prices are USD unless marked £ / € / ₹ / A$. "PEPM" means per employee per month. "Quote only" means the vendor publishes no number. Anything marked **est.** comes from a third party, not from the vendor.

### Corrections to §4 of the earlier file
- **Justworks PEO Basic is $79 PEPM** on the vendor page as reported by hireinsouth and casrai, Aug 2026. The earlier file said ~$59, which is an older figure. There is also a new standalone **Payroll plan at $50/mo + $8 PEPM**.
- **Remote EOR is $699/mo** on the vendor page. The "$599 annual" figure is no longer shown on remote.com; it only appears in older third-party write-ups.
- **Deel HR free tier: sources conflict.** One says it is free up to 200 employees if you use Deel EOR/Payroll, then $5 PEPM (pin.com, compono). costbench says the free plan was discontinued and HR is $5 PEPM. The vendor page does not show a free tier. **Treat it as $5 PEPM.**
- **Freshteam:** renewals stopped **7 Mar 2026**, full shutdown **Apr 2027**. Last prices were $1 / $2 / $4 PEPM billed annually ($1.20 / $2.40 / $4.80 monthly) plus a flat platform fee.
- **Zenefits / TriNet HR Plus:** self-serve tiers were withdrawn in Jan 2025 and it is now quote only. Historical prices: Essentials ~$10, Growth ~$20, Zen ~$27–33 PEPM.
- **Velocity Global has rebranded to Pebl.** The URL velocityglobal.com/pricing now redirects to hellopebl.com, which returns 404.

## 4. Pricing-model patterns
1. **Enterprise HCM (Workday, SAP, Oracle, UKG, Dayforce, ADP WFN, Paycom, Paylocity, Paycor) is quote only.** Buyers see $15–50 PEPM, multi-year terms, implementation of 10–150% of year 1, and renewal escalators of 3–10%. The opacity itself is a pain point we can exploit.
2. **"Base fee + PEPM" is the SMB norm** (Gusto $49–180, Rippling $35–40, Justworks $50, Paycor $99–299, ADP RUN ~$79, Paychex $39–95). At 10 employees the base fee doubles the effective price.
3. **Flat headcount bands** (BambooHR ≤25 = $250, Breathe, CharlieHR, Workable 1–20 = $299, greytHR base covering 50 or 10 employees) mean small teams pay $10–30 per head even when the list PEPM is low.
4. **Modular "platform + module" pricing** (Rippling, HiBob, Factorial, Personio, Leapsome, Omni, Sage HR, SAP) keeps the advertised floor at $3–10 while the real bundle is 2–4× that.
5. **Hard minimums**: Lattice $4k/yr, Leapsome €199/mo, Employment Hero 10 users plus per-add-on monthly minimums, Zoho 5 users, Culture Amp Vendr floor ~$7.7k/yr.
6. **Annual-only lock-in is common** (Rippling, Lattice, 15Five, Culture Amp, Leapsome, Omni, Personio, HiBob). **Monthly, no contract** is a differentiator: Gusto, Justworks, Deel, Remote, BambooHR, Breathe, Charlie.
7. **Global and EOR tier**: EOR costs $400 (Multiplier) to $770 (Papaya); the anchor is $599–699 (Deel, Remote, Oyster, Rippling, Pebl). Own-entity global payroll clusters at **$29 PEPM** (Deel, Remote) and $20–40 elsewhere. Contractors cost $29–49. Hidden FX (1–3%), deposits and setup fees sit on top.
8. **Performance and engagement are sold per module at $4–16** (Lattice, 15Five, Culture Amp, Leapsome). Nobody below $4 bundles performance, except Zoho ($3 Premium), Omni (+$2), greytHR ME (+$2–3) and Sage (+$2).
9. **The low-cost floor already exists in emerging markets**: Zoho People $1.25–4.50, Omni $3–7, greytHR ME $2–3 (+$50–60 base), greytHR India ₹45–85 PEPM, Freshteam $1–4 (now dead). These are YukthiX's real comparables on price. Western "per product" tools start at ~$4.
10. **SSO and API gating**: greytHR ME charges +$0.50 for SSO and +$1 for API; Humaans puts SSO in Enterprise only; Personio puts API in Enterprise; Odoo puts API in Custom. Culture Amp and Leapsome include SSO on every plan. Including SSO and API at $1 is a clear wedge.
11. **Price hikes are recent and visible**: Gusto Simple base +22% (Mar 2026), Paycor and TriNet pulled public pricing, Factorial "doubled" at renewal (review). A published, locked price is a message in its own right.
12. **Per-user vs per-employee**: Odoo charges per *login user* (employee records are free), which is very cheap for admin-only HR. Frappe HR is free software with $5–40/mo hosting.

### Takeaways for YukthiX ($1 per employee per month per product, all features)
- On list price, HR + payroll together (2 × $1 = $2) is **below every Western vendor at every size**. Gusto Simple at 1,000 employees is $6.05; Justworks Payroll is $8.05.
- It is also **at or below greytHR ME ($2.03–3.03 at scale)**. It is under Omni ($5–11) and Zoho ($1.25–4.50 for HR only, payroll extra).
- It cannot undercut self-hosted Frappe HR or admin-only Odoo on raw cost; there the competition is on service and features.
- **At 10 employees** the gap is widest, because base fees and bands dominate: Gusto $20, BambooHR $32, Paycor $32, Lattice $33 vs YukthiX $1–4. Keep a "no base fee, no minimum" promise.
- Things to price in or say plainly: country payroll coverage (competitors charge $29 for global payroll), SSO and API (greytHR ME charges +$1.50), and implementation (competitors charge $1k–$2.5M).

### Gaps and caveats
- The vendor pages for Gusto, Rippling, Justworks, Papaya, Multiplier, Sage, Personio (429) and Breathe (404) could not be fetched. Their numbers come from multiple agreeing aggregators and are marked M.
- The Zoho People vendor page did not render prices. The tinyteam figures (Mar 2026) are used.
- Zoho Payroll, Gusto Global and Paychex current tier prices could not be confirmed.
- Enterprise ranges are buyer-reported benchmarks, not list prices. Real negotiated prices at 1,000+ employees are often 30–40% lower.
