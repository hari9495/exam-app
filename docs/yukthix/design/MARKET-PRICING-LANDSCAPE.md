> **Date:** 27 Sep 2026. **Scope:** about 170 vendors across HRMS and payroll (India and global), ATS and staffing software (India and global), coding and skills assessment, psychometrics and video interviews, and exam proctoring.

## 1. Are all these products in India and worldwide?

| Category | India market | Worldwide market |

| **ATS** | Job boards dominate (Naukri, foundit, Apna, WorkIndia, Indeed). **Nobody sells all three (HRMS + ATS + proctoring) as one product with one price.** Darwinbox and Keka have HRMS + ATS; Workable and BambooHR have ATS + light HR; Mettl and HackerRank are assessment-only. That combination is YukthiX's structural advantage.

## 2. How the market prices

| Pattern | Who uses it | What it does to the buyer |
|---|---|---|
| **Base fee covering 25–100 employees, then per employee** | greytHR (50), Keka (100), factoHR, Pocket, Superworks, Kredily (25), Zoho Payroll; Gusto, Rippling, Justworks base fees | A 10-person company pays for 50–100 people: ₹450–1,600 per employee |
| **Minimum billing of 40–50 users** | HROne, Zimyo, Qandle, Pocket | Same effect as a base fee |
| **Tiered plans with modules locked to higher tiers** | Almost everyone | Performance, expense, geofence, analytics, SSO and API push buyers up a tier |
| **Paid add-ons per module** | greytHR (PMS ₹35–45, expense ₹35, GPS ₹140, SSO ₹10, API ₹15), Zimyo (Recruit ₹4,000, Analytics ₹20,000), Rippling, HiBob, Factorial | The advertised price is 2–4× lower than the real bundle |
| **Recruitment sold per recruiter as an add-on** | Keka ₹1,500–2,500, greytHR ₹2,500, Zimyo ₹4,000, sumHR ₹999, factoHR $75 | ATS costs 10–40× more per seat than YukthiX |
| **Priced by total company headcount** | Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, iCIMS, Workable, BambooHR | The ATS bill grows with employees, not recruiting activity |
| **Annual bundles of credits that expire, plus $20–30 overage** | HackerRank, CodeSignal, Codility, CoderPad, Testlify, TestGorilla, Adaface | Unused credits are lost; real cost per test is $12–30 |
| **Per exam, priced by duration** | Examity, ProctorU, Honorlock, Proctortrack | Live proctoring $11–33 per exam |
| **Quote only** | Darwinbox, PeopleStrong, Workday, SAP, Oracle, Greenhouse, Lever, Mettl, Talview, HireVue, Wheebox | No comparison possible; implementation fees from ₹10k to 150 % of year one |
| **Free tiers that are crippled or withdrawn** | greytHR ≤25 (250 MB), Zoho ≤5–10, Kredily (ads), RazorpayX (withdrawn) | Trust breaks when the free tier changes |

**YukthiX is the only product priced per person with no seat floor, no tiers and no module add-ons, across all three categories.**

**What this tells us**
1. **The biggest price gaps are in ATS (10–99×), assessment (17–24×) and small companies (5–17×).** These are the easiest markets to capture on price alone.
2. **HRMS for mid-size Indian companies is only 1.3–2.6× cheaper.** Keka, HROne, Qandle and greytHR are already close at 200 employees. Here YukthiX must win on "everything included" (recruitment, performance, geofence, SSO, API, multi-entity with no add-ons) and on the quality issues in the market analysis, not on price alone.
3. **The bundle is the killer offer: 4–5× cheaper** than buying HRMS, ATS and assessment from the usual vendors, and one vendor, one login, one data model.
4. **A few products are cheaper than YukthiX on raw price, but each leaves a lot out:**
   - **Kredily ₹70 and sumHR ₹49–69:** one salary structure or limited modules, and add-ons extra.
   - **greytHR Growth ₹85 without add-ons:** performance, expense, GPS, SSO and API are all extra.
   - **Frappe / Odoo self-hosted:** you run and patch it yourself; India statutory is incomplete; there is no support.
   - **AutoProctor ₹9:** AI-only, with no live proctoring; ID and recording are extra.
   - **Video-only subscriptions:** cheaper than $1 only above about 3,000–4,000 interviews a year.

## 6. Risks to watch

- **Price as a quality signal.** At 10–100× cheaper, some buyers will assume the product is weak. Counter with ISO 27001 and SOC 2, the pen-test summary, public status page, named pilot customers and published uptime. This is also why the founder's goal of market capture needs visible proof points, not just a low price.
- **Live proctoring cost.** Competitors charge $11–33 for a live proctored hour. The $1 covers live proctoring **by the company's own invigilators** (T04). YukthiX-provided proctors are a paid add-on. Say this plainly on the pricing page so no one expects human proctors from YukthiX at $1.
- **Heavy use by a few customers.** Coding workspaces, long video, and AI can cost more than $1 for heavy users. The fair-use limits and add-ons in D18 and YX-BILL-09 must be enforced from day one.
- **Competitor response.** greytHR and Kredily could cut prices; Zoho could bundle. The defence is the bundle (three products in one), the editable-rules model, and the staffing and campus features nobody else has.
- **Data quality.** Some competitor prices come from trackers and competitor blogs. Recheck each figure on the vendor site before using it publicly, as the analysis files mark.
