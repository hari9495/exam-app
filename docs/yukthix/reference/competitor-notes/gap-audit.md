## Top 10 for an Indian mid-size company's daily use (audit's view)
1. **Partial cancel or extend of leave, plus calendar sync:** employees change plans every week.
2. **Approval desk layout and archive:** managers clear approvals every day.
3. **Late arrivals, least hours and breaks view:** late marks drive pay cuts and discipline.
4. **Office or remote on each punch:** hybrid rules need proof for each day.
5. **Bulk shift and weekly-off changes with import:** factories and BPOs move hundreds of people every month.
6. **Recycle bin and backups across YukthiX:** wrong deletes can't be undone today outside hiring.
7. **Sign in again before salary pages:** shared office computers.
8. **Reports library with schedules:** HR sends the same monthly MIS reports again and again.
9. **Editable leave balance grid:** corrections at go-live and at year start.
10. **Timesheet approval drill-down:** delivery managers approve every week.
